It all kicks off tonight, and GTA 6 brings several systems that work differently from GTA 5. So the police don't catch you off guard in your first hours, and your getaway car isn't worthless by the time it reaches the fence, we've summed up what Rockstar showed in the Extended Look and what the press reported from the previews.

**As of November 18, 2026.** Important: the game isn't out yet. Everything here comes from the August Extended Look and from previews. The final version may differ in the details. We'll check these tips after launch and update the guide.

## 1. Wanted level: six stars and no cops on the minimap

The wanted level goes up to **six stars** again, after five in GTA 5. What's new above all is how the police get their information:

- The police are only alerted if someone sees the crime or a security system detects it.
- Icons under the stars show what the police know about you, such as your face, your clothing or whether you're armed.
- Outlined stars mean there's no confirmed description of you yet.
- Cops no longer appear on the minimap. You find them with the Focus feature.
- At one star, losing the police is easy. At six stars, an all-out manhunt begins.

**Our tip:** Before every job, take a quick look for witnesses and cameras. When things get hot, alleys, switching vehicles, changing clothes, a new haircut or a mask can help.

## 2. Criminal Profile: the game remembers how you behave

Instead of a simple good-versus-evil system, GTA 6 tracks a **Criminal Profile**. It takes the place of the Honor system from Red Dead Redemption 2, but according to previews it works differently:

- Unnecessary violence and excessive destruction count against you.
- Violence that a mission or robbery requires doesn't hurt your profile.
- Clean, efficient crimes build Jason and Lucia's reputation as capable criminals.
- Even small things count: throwing trash in a bin can improve your profile.

**Our tip:** Don't go on a rampage in your first hour. Ruining your profile early just makes life harder for no reason.

## 3. Stealing cars: scan first, then break in

Vehicles have **security tiers**, and expensive models are much harder to steal.

- With the **WAINK** phone app, you scan a car beforehand: its value, registration cost, lock status, alarm and tracking tech.
- You can open older cars with simple tools like a slim jim.
- Modern luxury cars need key-cloning tools. Some of that gear only arrives later in the story.
- Some cars have **GPS trackers** that tell the police where you are. Then you either outrun the signal or ditch the car.
- Damaged cars fetch less money at the fence.

**Our tip:** Start with older cars and scan before you steal. And drive carefully if you plan to sell.

## 4. Everyday vehicles and getting around

- **Trunk:** Cars have a usable trunk for weapons and spare clothes.
- **Your own vehicles:** You can own cars and park them at your home.
- **Swapping seats:** When Jason and Lucia drive together, they can swap seats. One drives, the other fends off pursuers.
- **Fast travel:** The rideshare app **Ryde Me** gets you around quickly. When Jason and Lucia share a ride, they chat with the other passengers.
- **On the water and in the air:** Jet skis, kayaks, scuba diving and parachuting were among the things shown.

With the Ultimate Edition, Jason's safehouse also gets a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak.

**Our tip:** Put spare clothes in your trunk early. During a chase, a quick outfit change can make the difference.

## 5. Jason and Lucia: switching and sticking together

- When they're together, you switch between them instantly. Apart, it takes a few seconds.
- The character you're not controlling follows their own schedule and sends you texts.
- Ignoring those texts all the time can hurt the relationship.
- One can be arrested while the other escapes.

**Our tip:** Answer the texts. According to previews, joint robberies also strengthen the bond between the two.

## 6. Money: robbing, selling, cracking safes

According to previews, money matters more to the story. Some progress requires a set amount of cash.

- **Income:** Stealing and selling cars, robbing stores and businesses, bank heists.
- **Fences:** You sell stolen goods to specialized fences.
- **Safes:** Blowing them open is quick, but can damage the valuables. Finding the combination takes longer, but keeps the full value.
- **Timing:** Linger at the scene and you risk the police showing up. Many locations have a second exit.

**Our tip:** When things are quiet, going for the combination pays off.

## 7. Aiming and combat

- Free aim in a more modern, cover-based third-person style.
- Lucia was shown shooting from the hip while carrying a bag.
- A Dead Eye-style slow-motion mode highlights weak spots like heads, hands and joints.

## 8. Phone, Focus and downtime

- **Phone:** It's a physical object in the game world, and the camera moves to the character's hand when you use it.
- **Snapmatic:** Rockstar's Instagram parody. You can browse posts and follow accounts, but you can't upload photos yourself.
- **Focus:** Highlights nearby objects and helps you avoid the police.
- **3D minimap:** Nearby buildings appear as 3D models.
- **Downtime:** Pool, mini golf, the zoo, working out, races and a wildlife checklist.

## 9. Home and style

- Jason and Lucia share a base, similar to the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2.
- You can change your hair at home, but new hairstyles require a barber or salon.
- According to previews, clothing ranges from tank tops to sequin dresses.

## No real money in story mode

Rob Nelson, co-studio head of Rockstar North, told Kinda Funny: no monetization in the game. So there are no real-money purchases in story mode. Asked about generative AI, he also said no.

## Our six quick tips for launch

1. **Check for witnesses and cameras** before you make a move.
2. **Keep spare clothes in the trunk.**
3. **Steal older cars first** and scan them beforehand.
4. **Deliver undamaged to the fence**, or the price drops.
5. **Answer texts**, the relationship matters.
6. **Look after your profile:** no unnecessary violence, and pick up some trash now and then.
