There are hundreds of GTA 5 easter eggs, but few are as legendary as the UFO on Mount Chiliad or the ghost on Mount Gordo. In this guide you'll find the best-known secret locations in Story Mode, with the exact spot, the requirements and the right in-game time.

**As of November 17, 2026.** Applies to Story Mode on PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S.

## The Mount Chiliad mystery

### The mural

Inside the cable car station at the very top of Mount Chiliad hangs a mural that has kept the community busy since 2013. Among other things, it shows a UFO, an egg and a figure with a jetpack. Many players spent years hunting for the solution. Rockstar did actually add the UFO, but there is no jetpack in Story Mode.

### The UFO above the summit

Once you reach **100% completion**, a UFO appears above the summit of Mount Chiliad. The conditions:

- Time: around 3 a.m. in-game time
- Weather: thunderstorm
- Location: right on the summit, ideally wait near the cable car station

**Tip:** You can give the weather a nudge with the weather cheat from our [GTA 5 cheats list](/en/cheats/gta-5/). Keep in mind that cheats disable achievements and trophies for the current session.

### Two more UFOs

After 100%, two more UFOs hover over San Andreas:

- **Fort Zancudo:** high above the control tower. The side reads "segregate and rearrange", a hint that "segregate" is an anagram of "easter egg".
- **Sandy Shores:** high above the desert near Sandy Shores.

The easiest way to reach both is by plane or helicopter.

## The ghost on Mount Gordo

One of the creepiest moments in GTA 5:

- **Location:** on the cliffs of Mount Gordo in the northeast of the map
- **Time:** between 11 p.m. and midnight in-game time
- **What you'll see:** a floating female figure, with the word **JOCK** written in blood on the rock below

Get too close and she vanishes, and at midnight the haunting is over completely. The ghost is **Jolene Cranley-Evans**. Clues in the game suggest that her husband Jock Cranley, a former stuntman turned politician, pushed her off the cliff during a trip. Officially, it was ruled an accident.

## Bigfoot: The Last One

The Bigfoot myth runs through the whole series, and in GTA 5 you finally get him in your sights.

- **Mission:** The Last One
- **Requirement:** 100% completion, Franklin only
- **Start:** Raton Canyon, where a hunter sends you on the hunt

The mission doesn't always show up right after you hit 100%. Be patient and keep playing as Franklin for a while. A surprise awaits at the end: Bigfoot is a man in a costume. Whether you spare him is up to you.

**Bonus:** Even earlier, in the mission **Predator**, you can briefly spot a Bigfoot silhouette at night through the thermal scope. It disappears after a few seconds.

## The sunken UFO

Off the coast of **Procopio Beach**, east of North Point near Paleto Bay, a crashed UFO lies on the seabed.

- You need a submarine to take a proper look at it.
- It doesn't count toward 100% completion, but it's a must-see for every UFO fan.

## The Altruist Camp

The Altruists are a cult holed up in the **Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness**. As Trevor, you can pick up hitchhikers during random events and deliver them straight to the camp instead of their destination. After several deliveries, the mood turns and a shootout breaks out. So come well armed.

## The frozen alien

Even the North Yankton prologue hides an easter egg: a frozen alien lies in the iced-over river beneath the railway bridge. You can only see it during the prologue missions in Ludendorff, so keep your eyes peeled.

## The Leonora Johnson murder case

An easter egg for collectors: 50 letter scraps are scattered across Los Santos and Blaine County. Piece them together and you get a killer's confession to an old, unsolved case involving Leonora Johnson. Once you've found all the scraps, you can confront the killer in a short encounter of their own. Perfect for anyone who wants a real crime story alongside the main plot.

## Small extra: the loyal dog

Between 10 a.m. and 2 p.m. in-game time, a dog sits in front of a grave at the entrance of the Hill Valley Church cemetery in Pacific Bluffs. It's a nod to the story of Greyfriars Bobby, the Scottish dog that guarded his owner's grave for years.

## How to find everything

- **100% first:** Many secrets require full completion.
- **Skip time:** Sleeping in a safehouse cuts down the waiting.
- **Save:** Create a separate save before experimenting with cheats.
