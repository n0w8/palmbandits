Right before launch, plenty of people are still asking: is the Standard Edition enough, or is the Ultimate Edition worth the extra $20? Here's everything in each edition, what the pre-order bonus is all about and why the most expensive GTA 6 box doesn't contain a game at all.

**As of November 18, 2026**

## The editions compared

| | Standard Edition | Ultimate Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Price (US) | $79.99 | $99.99 |
| Price (Europe) | €79.99 | €99.99 |
| Base game | Yes | Yes |
| 16 extra items | Available as an upgrade | Included |
| Boxed retail copy | Yes, with a download code | No, digital only |
| Vintage Vice City Pack | If bought before Nov 20 | If bought before Nov 20 |

Take-Two confirmed the US prices in June.

## All 16 Ultimate Edition items

Rockstar describes the Ultimate Edition as "an exclusive collection of items threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story." According to VGC, it includes:

**Vehicles**

- '95 Grotti Cheetah, a sports car
- Jason's safehouse vehicles: a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak
- '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy plus the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay
- Shitzu Squalo, a watercraft docked at Washington Beach, with a weapons crate full of explosives
- Ganado Retro Build, a mod kit for Jason's Vapid Ganado
- Classic Car Collection, a special commission from Wyman

**Weapons**

- Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, a his-and-hers pair
- Personalized pistols: Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17

**Looks**

- Vice City Style: outfits, tattoos and more
- Goodtime Gear, an apparel capsule

**Shops**

- Rideout Customs, a mod shop
- One-Eyed Willie's, another mod shop
- Sara's Unisex Salon for hair, facial hair, makeup and nails
- Stock 305, a clothing store
- Electric Fang Tattoo with over 50 tattoos designed by FAILE
- PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store in a gang compound

Important: five of these shops, Rideout Customs, Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo and One-Eyed Willie's, are only open for business with the Ultimate Edition, according to VGC.

**Not everything at once:** According to Vice, you unlock the items gradually as you progress through the story. The Grotti Cheetah, for example, only becomes available later in the game.

## Upgrade: possible at any time

You don't have to decide tonight. According to Rockstar Support, as quoted by Vice, you can buy the upgrade later through PlayStation or Xbox. That also applies if you bought the boxed version and redeemed the code. Take-Two also confirmed during its quarterly results that the add-on content can be purchased separately.

## Vintage Vice City Pack: release day is the last day

All pre-orders and purchases made **before November 20, 2026** get the Vintage Vice City Pack, whichever edition. So if you buy on release day, you still qualify. According to Vice, it includes:

- a '55 Vapid Stanier plus a garage
- outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia
- an exclusive weapon pattern
- one month of GTA+

Boxed copies of the Standard Edition include the pack too, but only while supplies last. The GTA+ month is handled separately by the digital storefronts, so if you're buying the box, check the retailer listing.

## Boxed version: no disc

The retail box contains **no disc**, just a download code. Rockstar confirmed this back in June. Only the Standard Edition is sold as a box, the Ultimate Edition is digital only.

## The Vice City Collection: a collector's box without the game

Since September 24, you've been able to pre-order a collector's box on the Rockstar Store: **GTA 6: The Goodtime State, Vice City Collection**. The catch: there's no game inside. Price according to Push Square: $399.99 or £349.99. Our sources don't list a euro price. It's due to ship from November 19, and quantities are limited.

According to Push Square, it includes, among other things:

- a Macca the Gator figure with a stash compartment in the base
- Oakley sunglasses
- a Leonida Keys crossbody bag
- a New Era cap
- a magnetic mirror, a shot glass, pins, stickers and a keychain
- a double-sided poster with a map of Leonida

## Which edition is right for whom?

This is our take:

**Standard is enough if you...**

- mainly want to experience the story. Nothing in the official descriptions suggests you need the Ultimate items for it.
- want the box on your shelf. It's only available as Standard.
- want to try the game first. The upgrade isn't going anywhere.

**Ultimate is worth it if you...**

- love modding cars, styling your characters and trying out shops. Five shops only open with Ultimate.
- have your eye on vehicles like the Grotti Cheetah or the Dominator Buggy.
- already know you're going to squeeze every last drop out of Leonida.

One thing we like: according to Rob Nelson of Rockstar North, there's no monetization in the game, so no real-money purchases in story mode. That makes the extra $20 for Ultimate a one-time, clearly defined decision.

We'd steer anyone who just wants to play away from the collector's box. It's a merch package for collectors, not part of the game.
