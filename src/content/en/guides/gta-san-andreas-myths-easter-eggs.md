GTA San Andreas myths sent a whole generation out hunting: nights in the fog of Back o' Beyond, chasing Bigfoot or a chainsaw killer. In this guide we draw a clear line between myth and reality and show you the real easter eggs you can still find today.

**As of November 17, 2026.** Applies to the original and, for the most part, to the Definitive Edition as well.

## The big myths

### Myth: Bigfoot

The ultimate classic. Bigfoot supposedly roams the foggy forests around Back o' Beyond and Mount Chiliad. Thousands of players searched for him there at night.

**The truth:** Bigfoot was never found, not even by digging through the game files. The famous photos come from mods or are simply fakes. Rockstar later picked up on the myth: in GTA 5, Bigfoot got an appearance of his own. More on that in our guide to [GTA 5 easter eggs](/en/guides/gta-5-secret-locations-easter-eggs/).

### Myth: Leatherface

A man with a chainsaw supposedly lurks in the woods at night, waiting for CJ. The atmosphere in Back o' Beyond fits a story like that perfectly.

**The truth:** There is no such character. The chainsaw is a perfectly normal weapon in the game, but a horror killer belongs in the realm of campfire stories.

### Phenomenon: ghost cars

Empty cars that seem to roll off on their own or glide across the road. Lots of players really have seen this, often in hilly areas.

**The truth:** The phenomenon is real, but the usual explanation is completely unspectacular: game physics. Vehicles that spawn on slopes or get stuck while spawning start moving without a driver. There are no ghosts behind the wheel.

### Myth: UFOs over Area 69

Area 69 is real, a heavily guarded military base in Bone County. In the mission Black Project, CJ even breaks into it. Also in Bone County is the Lil' Probe Inn, a bar decked out entirely in alien style. No wonder players claim to have seen UFOs in the night sky.

**The truth:** There are no flying UFOs in San Andreas. The alien references are decoration and humor, not hidden spaceships.

### Myth: the lake monster

A Loch Ness-style monster is said to live in the lakes out in the countryside.

**The truth:** No monster, no traces in the code. A typical schoolyard myth from a time when everyone knew someone who had supposedly seen it.

### Real place, made-up haunting: Las Brujas

Las Brujas is an abandoned settlement in Bone County. The name translates to "the witches", and the area is bleak and empty. The perfect breeding ground for ghost stories.

**The truth:** The place is real and genuinely feels creepy. But there are no ghosts or hidden events there.

## Real easter eggs you can find

### The sign on the Gant Bridge

Fly to the very top of the Gant Bridge in San Fierro by jetpack, helicopter or plane, and you'll find a sign on the bridge tower that reads:

> "There are no easter eggs up here. Go away."

A wonderfully dry greeting to everyone who searches every corner for secrets.

### Bridge facts at the visitor center

At the Gant Bridge visitor center, there's an information board with facts about the bridge. Among them is a note on how much disc space the bridge takes up. Classic Rockstar humor.

### A trip to Liberty City

In the mission **Saint Mark's Bistro** for Salvatore Leone, CJ flies to Liberty City, the city from GTA 3. There, you carry out a job in the restaurant of the same name. You can't drive around Liberty City freely, but the reunion is a real highlight for series fans.

### A jab at the competition

In San Fierro there are gravestones with the inscription "RIP Opposition". This is widely seen as a jab from Rockstar at rival games, especially Driver 3.

### Catalina's graves

Behind Catalina's hideout in the countryside lie three graves with a shovel next to them. Rockstar never explains it, but the hint is clear: Catalina's previous partners were apparently less lucky than CJ. A small and pretty dark detail that many players completely miss on their first playthrough.

### Hot Coffee: the story behind the scandal

Not an easter egg to hunt for, but part of the legend: the San Andreas game files contained an unfinished, adults-only minigame. In 2005, a PC mod made it accessible, the outrage was huge, and the game's age rating in the US was changed. Rockstar then released a cleaned-up version of the game in which the minigame can no longer be accessed.
