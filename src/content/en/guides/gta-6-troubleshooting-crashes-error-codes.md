Since its November 19 launch, GTA 6 has run fine for most people on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Some players are hitting crashes, stuck downloads or sign-in trouble, though. Here is what Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft officially say and what you can try yourself.

**As of November 20, 2026, 8 a.m. CET.** According to PC Games Hardware, Rockstar has not yet explained the reported crashes or released a fix for them.

## The short answer

- **Error codes:** Rockstar does not keep its own list. Its GTA VI support page points you to the PlayStation and Xbox error code lookups.
- **CE-108255-1 (PS5):** Sony calls it a general error while running a game. Its advice: update the game.
- **Crashes:** The cause is still unknown. Save manually and often.
- **Connection:** Check the official status pages first, then your own network.

## Who helps with what

According to Sony's GTA VI support page, Rockstar handles problems inside the game, patches and bugs. PlayStation Support covers your account, launch and download trouble, codes, installation and digital refunds. A missing or unreadable code in a physical box is a job for the store you bought it from.

## Look up your error code

1. Write the code down exactly, or snap a photo of the screen.
2. Enter it in the PlayStation or Xbox error code search. Rockstar links both in its article.
3. Follow the steps there. If it is a bug in the game itself, report it to Rockstar Support.

### CE-108255-1 on PS5

PC Games Hardware says this code keeps coming up in GTA 6 threads. Sony calls it a general error with a game or app and recommends updating: highlight GTA 6 in your Game Library, press Options and pick Check for Update. Whether that fixes the current problems is not confirmed, PCGH notes.

## GTA 6 won't download or start (PS5)

Sony has a dedicated help page for GTA VI. Here are its steps:

1. **Stuck download:** In the control center, go to Downloads/Uploads, pick GTA 6 and hit Pause, then Resume. If that does nothing, choose Cancel and Delete and download the game again from your Game Library.
2. **Status page:** If PlayStation's status page shows an outage, wait.
3. **System software:** In Settings, go to System, then System Software, and open the update options there. GTA 6 will not start without the latest version.
4. **Storage:** Select the game in your library, then More and View Product, to see the space it needs. Settings > Storage shows what you have free.
5. **Rebuild the database:** Boot the PS5 in Safe Mode and choose Clear Cache and Rebuild Database.
6. **Different account:** If you are not playing on the account that bought the game, that account needs Console Sharing and Offline Play turned on for this PS5. You find it in Settings under Users and Accounts, then Other.
7. **Licenses:** In the same menu, pick Restore Licenses.

## Xbox Series X|S

We found no GTA 6 help page from Microsoft, and Rockstar sends Xbox players to the Xbox error code search. Beyond that, general tips that are not specific to GTA 6:

1. Check the Xbox services status page.
2. Fully restart the console instead of just waking it from sleep.
3. Under Manage game and add-ons, open Updates to make sure you are on the latest version.

## Crashes and lost saves

PC Games Hardware reports crashes on PS5 and Xbox Series X, often in the prologue, plus missions that refuse to progress. How many players are affected is unclear, and PC Games logged 65 review hours without a single crash. To protect your progress:

1. **Save manually:** Notebookcheck says the prologue and some mission sections do not trigger an autosave.
2. **Check Load Game:** Autosaves are not listed in the save menu, Notebookcheck says. A save that looks lost may still be in the load menu.
3. **Don't pull the plug:** A sudden power cut may corrupt a save, Notebookcheck suggests. Quit through the menu.
4. **Stuck mission:** In one case PCGH cites, only restarting the mission helped.

## Connection problems

On November 18, XDA reported that many PS5 players could not sign in to PSN and were told the services were busy, while Sony's status page showed everything as working. A green status page is no guarantee. In general:

1. Wait a few minutes, then sign in again.
2. Restart your router and console.
3. Use a wired connection instead of Wi-Fi if you can.

## Settings: where to find what

Rockstar says you open the Pause Menu and select Settings. Its support article lists these sections:

- **Control Scheme and Controller:** Layouts for on foot, aiming, vehicles and more, plus sensitivity, inverted controls and DualSense features.
- **Gameplay:** Combat, movement and accessibility.
- **Display:** Picture options, effects and a high-contrast shader option.
- **UI:** Scaling, subtitles, navigation, reticle, HUD notifications and units.
- **Camera and Audio:** Camera on foot, in vehicles and in the air, plus volume and narration.

Restore All or Restore Default at the bottom of the screen undoes your changes. There is no graphics mode for higher FPS.
