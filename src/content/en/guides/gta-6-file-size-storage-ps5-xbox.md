For a lot of players, GTA 6 is the biggest game that has ever landed on their console. It wants around 212 GB tonight, and depending on your model, that gets tight fast. Here's how much space you need, how much your console really has free, and the quickest ways to clear room.

**As of November 18, 2026, 5 p.m. CET.** Rockstar hasn't announced another patch for launch day. If one drops, we'll add its size here.

## The short answer

- **PS5 and PS5 Pro:** at least 212 GB free, according to Sony's official page for the game.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** around 200 GB, per Windows Central. Rockstar itself says up to 212 GB across consoles.
- **On top of that:** two pre-launch updates, plus a mandatory system update on PS5.
- **Our rule of thumb:** clear 250 GB. That leaves room for future patches.

## The numbers at a glance

| What | PS5 / PS5 Pro | Xbox Series X\|S |
|---|---|---|
| Game | at least 212 GB free (Sony) | around 200 GB (Windows Central) |
| Update 1.002.000 (Nov 13) | about 1.07 GB | unconfirmed, about 2.2 GB |
| Update 1.002.001 (from Nov 16) | about 602 MB | about 1.6 GB |
| System update | required before first launch | nothing reported |

Rockstar hasn't published patch notes for either update. The sizes come from trackers that read store data and from reports such as Tech Times. Update 1.002.000 is flagged as mandatory, so the game won't start without it.

## Will GTA 6 fit on your console?

What matters isn't the number on the box but the usable space, since the system reserves part of the SSD. We're using 212 GB for the game:

| Console | SSD | usable | free after GTA 6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PS5 with 825 GB | 825 GB | about 667 GB | about 455 GB |
| PS5 Slim with 1 TB | 1 TB | about 848 GB | about 636 GB |
| PS5 Pro | 2 TB | about 1.89 TB | about 1.68 TB |
| Xbox Series X | 1 TB | about 802 GB | about 590 GB |
| Xbox Series S | 512 GB | about 364 GB | about 152 GB |

The usable figures come from measurements by Push Square, GameSpot and The FPS Review. Anything else you have installed comes off that, of course. On the **512 GB Series S**, that means GTA 6 plus one or two smaller games, and that's it.

## How to free up space on PS5

1. **Check what's using space:** Settings > Storage > Console Storage shows you what takes up how much.
2. **Move PS5 games out:** Highlight a game in your Game Library, press Options and choose "Move Games and Apps". PS5 games are safe on a USB drive but can't be played from there. Copying them back is usually faster than downloading them again.
3. **Keep PS4 games on USB:** Sony says they run straight from external storage, so you save space without deleting anything.
4. **Clean out the Media Gallery:** Clips and screenshots add up to several gigabytes quickly.

Sony's requirements for the USB drive: SuperSpeed USB 5 Gbps or faster and 250 GB to 8 TB of capacity. USB hubs aren't supported.

## More room for GTA 6 itself: an M.2 SSD in your PS5

If you want to install GTA 6 somewhere other than the internal SSD, an M.2 SSD in the expansion slot is the only way. Sony's requirements:

- **Interface:** PCIe Gen4 x4 NVMe. M.2 SATA drives don't work.
- **Capacity:** 250 GB to 8 TB.
- **Speed:** 5,500 MB/s sequential read or faster is recommended.
- **Cooling:** a heatsink is required, either built in or added.
- **Form factor:** 2230, 2242, 2260, 2280 or 22110.

After you install it, the PS5 formats the drive and you can move games onto it. If you're doing this tonight, check that the heatsink fits under the cover first. Sony warns that oversized heatsinks can stop it from closing.

## Xbox Series X|S: expansion card, not a USB drive

The same rule applies on Xbox in a different form. According to Microsoft, games optimized for Series X|S only run from the internal SSD or an official Storage Expansion Card, such as Seagate's. A USB 3.1 drive can store GTA 6 but can't launch it. Xbox One and Xbox 360 games, on the other hand, play directly from USB.

To make room, highlight a game in "My games & apps", go to "Manage" and move it to your USB drive. Older Xbox One games are the best candidates because you can keep playing them.

## Updates and system software before launch

- **PS5 system update:** According to PlayStation Support, quoted by Kotaku, Sony requires a new system update before GTA 6 will start. Sony hasn't said what it changes.
- **Day one patch:** Rockstar hasn't announced one. Some outlets already call 1.002.000 the day one patch because it has to be installed before you first play. After launch we'll check whether another one arrives.
- **Staggered rollout:** Rockstar doesn't push updates to everyone at once. If you don't have one yet, nothing is broken.

## Download tips for tonight

- **Check your version:** On PS5, highlight GTA 6, press Options and pick "Check for Update". On Xbox, open "Manage game and add-ons" and look under "Updates".
- **Rest mode with internet:** Your console will grab new patches on its own.
- **Cable over Wi-Fi:** An Ethernet cable is usually more stable for big downloads.
- **Don't reinstall:** If a download stalls, pause and resume it or restart the console. Deleting it can cost you a fresh 200 GB download.
- **Boxed copy:** There's no disc inside, just a download code. No redeemed code, no pre-load.

## Our tip

Clear the space now, not at 11:55 p.m. It's easiest on PS5 Pro and Series X and hardest on the 512 GB Series S, where it pays to decide which game you actually need over the next few weeks. A USB drive for cold storage is the cheapest fix, while an M.2 SSD or expansion card is the most convenient.
