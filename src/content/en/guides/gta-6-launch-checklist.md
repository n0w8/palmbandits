The GTA 6 launch checklist for everyone who doesn't want to waste a single minute on release night. Work through these points by Wednesday evening, and on November 19 all you'll have to do is pick up the controller.

**As of November 17, 2026**

## The short version

- [ ] At least 212 GB free, ideally more
- [ ] Preload finished
- [ ] Box code redeemed (if you bought the boxed version)
- [ ] Console in rest mode with internet
- [ ] Automatic updates turned on
- [ ] Spoiler filters set up on social media
- [ ] Snacks, drinks, a free evening

## 1. Free up storage space

Rockstar lists **up to 212 GB**. A day-one patch may come on top of that. Our advice: clear 250 GB or more to be safe.

- **PS5:** The game has to be on the internal SSD or an M.2 expansion drive. Move other PS5 games to a USB drive if you want to keep them.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Games run from the internal SSD or an official expansion card. You can move other games to USB drives.
- **Xbox Series S with 512 GB:** Things get really tight here. Expect to have very little else installed alongside GTA 6.

## 2. Check the preload

The preload has been live since **November 10**.

1. Open your game library.
2. Check whether GTA 6 has fully downloaded.
3. If the download is stuck, restart it in the download queue instead of deleting the game.

Important: The preload doesn't unlock anything early. It just makes sure you don't spend hours downloading at launch.

## 3. Boxed version: redeem the code

The boxed version contains **no disc**, just a download code.

- Redeem the code now so you can preload as well.
- PlayStation codes only work in the matching store region. If you're importing from abroad, check this first.
- The code is tied to your account. Once redeemed, you can't lend or resell it.

## 4. Updates and rest mode

According to reports, Rockstar already pushed small updates before release. To make sure you're not staring at a patch progress bar on Thursday:

- Turn on automatic updates in the system settings.
- Allow rest mode with an internet connection.
- Simply leave your console in rest mode the night before launch.

## 5. Release night

- **Time:** According to store listings, midnight local time on the night of November 18 to 19. Rockstar has not officially confirmed the time.
- **Busy servers:** According to Sony and Microsoft, GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game in the history of their stores. Expect PSN or Xbox Live to buckle briefly.
- **Don't panic:** If the game doesn't start right away, don't reinstall it. Wait a moment, restart your console and try again.

## 6. Prepare your settings

We'll only see which options GTA 6 itself offers at launch. But there are a few things you can sort out right now:

- **Calibrate HDR:** In the PS5 or Xbox system settings, so Vice City doesn't look washed out or too dark.
- **Put your TV in game mode:** Noticeably reduces input lag.
- **Audio:** Set up your headphones or soundbar properly in the system settings.
- **Set expectations:** GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on all consoles, including the PS5 Pro.

Once in the game, it's worth checking brightness, subtitles and camera sensitivity before you hit the road.

## 7. Controllers and accessories

Sounds obvious, but it can save your launch night:

- **Charge your controllers:** Ideally fully charge two controllers or keep a charging cable within reach.
- **Controller updates:** The PS5 offers DualSense firmware updates directly in the system settings. Xbox controllers are updated via the console's Xbox Accessories app.
- **Test your headset:** Check the audio once before things kick off at midnight.
- **Check your internet:** Is your router running stable? A LAN cable is often faster than Wi-Fi for big updates.

## 8. Avoid spoilers

From midnight on, clips and screenshots will flood the internet. Here's how to protect yourself:

- **X, Instagram, TikTok:** Mute terms like "GTA 6 ending", "GTA 6 story" or character names.
- **YouTube:** Avoid your recommendations and don't click on stream thumbnails.
- **Discord and Reddit:** Leave or mute spoiler channels.
- **Brief your friends:** Make it clear in the group chat that nobody posts story moments.

## 9. What to do first

No guide in the world knows every GTA 6 mechanic yet. So keep it down to earth:

- **Story first:** Let the opening surprise you instead of googling everything in advance.
- **Explore:** Vice City and Leonida are brand new, so take your time.
- **Save often:** Better one save too many than one too few.
- **Screenshots:** The Share button is your friend, and the community loves seeing your best shots.
- **Take breaks:** Launch night is a marathon, not a sprint.

## 10. Bonuses and fine print

- **Vintage Vice City Pack:** Applies to pre-orders and purchases before November 20. So if you buy on release day, you still get it.
- **GTA+ trial month:** Included with digital purchases on the PlayStation Store, according to the store listing. It renews automatically, so put a cancellation reminder in your calendar.
- **Ultimate upgrade:** According to Rockstar, you can buy it at any time. No reason to rush it now.
- **PC players:** A PC version has not been announced.
