Cars have always been half of what GTA is about. In GTA 6, though, they carry a lot more weight: they have security tiers, need fuel, take damage with real consequences, and can be registered and customized. Here's everything known about vehicles in GTA 6, from the reviews, the previews and the official editions.

**As of November 18, 2026, 5 p.m. CET.** Rockstar hasn't published a full vehicle list. After launch we'll check the numbers and fill in what's missing.

## The short answer

- **More than 100 unique cars**, according to Shacknews, plus bikes, boats, planes, helicopters and service vehicles.
- **You can drive and fly all of it:** Kotaku says Jason and Lucia can take the wheel of cars, boats, planes and helicopters.
- **What's new:** security tiers for theft, refueling and charging, damage that matters, paid registration and separate mod shops for each vehicle type.

## How many vehicles are there?

There's no official number. Shacknews counts more than 100 unique cars and leaves out planes, helicopters, boats, bikes and service vehicles. GamingTrend talks about several hundred vehicles that all handle differently. Fan databases already list more than 300 entries, but those come from trailers, screenshots and leaks, not a confirmed list.

## What you can drive, fly and pilot

| Type | What's known | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cars | from rust buckets to supercars, EVs included | reviews |
| Motorcycles | dirt bikes, choppers, enduros | Kotaku, Red Bull, Ultimate Edition |
| Boats | speedboats, jet skis, kayaks, airboats in the swamps, diving for treasure | Extended Look, Kotaku, Shacknews |

Kotaku describes a chase where he took a speedboat instead of the suggested dirt bike, and the game adjusted the mission objective to match.

## Spotted in the trailers

In August, Red Bull rounded up the vehicles visible in trailers and screenshots. Plenty of old favorites are in there, such as the Sultan and Tempesta among the cars, the Maverick and Buzzard helicopters, the Shamal and Nimbus planes, and the Squalo and Marquis boats. Airboats and ATVs are new. These names are Red Bull's identifications, not an official list from Rockstar.

## Stealing cars: tools by security tier

The basics are in our [tips for your first hours](/en/guides/gta-6-first-hours-tips-gameplay-systems/). The short version:

- **WAINK app:** Before a theft, it shows the security tier, the tool you need, tracker status, registration cost and resale value.
- **Tools:** smashing a window, a slim jim, and later a key cloner for expensive cars. Previews say you get that by doing jobs for fences.
- **Trackers:** High-end cars report your location to the police. Kotaku says they're also harder to modify and sell.
- **The result:** Kotaku spent a lot of time in rust buckets and unlocked sedans.

## Handling, damage and fuel

According to TheGamer, the driving sits somewhere between GTA 5 and GTA 4: more weight, more physics, more damage. Streets are narrower and busier, so you have to pay attention.

- **Damage that matters:** After a hard crash, GamingTrend says your car starts pulling left or right. PlayStation Universe reports tires coming off and drivers flying through the windshield.
- **Details:** Sports car brakes glow after high speed, and small bumps leave visible scratches.
- **Fuel and charging:** Cars need gas, EVs need power. Run dry and the car sputters to a stop. Previews say you hold R2 or RT for about ten seconds at the pump.
- **Repairs:** Gas stations and Pay 'n' Spray fix damaged cars.
- **Not too strict:** Shacknews calls fuel and engine damage fairly lenient.
- **Partner at the wheel:** GamingTrend says your partner's driving AI is dramatically better than in earlier games.

## Owning, registering, customizing

- **Registration:** You can register stolen street cars for a fee. Shacknews says this happens at Pay 'n' Spray, and previews say high-end models only become available later.
- **Garages:** You park your own cars at home or in garages.
- **Trunk:** Room for weapons, bags and clothes. Shacknews says you can even save outfits to a vehicle, and previews say you can hide in the trunk.
- **Customization:** Shacknews describes mod shops for each vehicle type, with upgrades inside and out. TheGamer talks about a ridiculous amount of modding options.

| Vehicle | Type | Edition |
|---|---|---|
| '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage near Ocean Beach | classic | Vintage Vice City Pack, purchase before Nov 20 |
| '95 Grotti Cheetah | sports car | Ultimate |
| Dinka Enduro and Crest kayak | bike and boat for Jason's safehouse | Ultimate |
| '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with Paradise Garage | buggy | Ultimate |
| Shitzu Squalo with an explosives crate | boat | Ultimate |
| Ganado Retro Build | custom kit for Jason's Vapid Ganado | Ultimate |

Vice says Ultimate content unlocks as you progress through the story, so the Cheetah, for example, only shows up later. Whether the upgrade is worth it is covered in our [editions comparison](/en/guides/gta-6-editions-standard-vs-ultimate/).

## Our tip

Don't get greedy early on. Older cars without trackers are easier to steal, easier to sell and give the police less to work with. Scan every car with WAINK first, get it to the fence in one piece and keep an eye on the tank. Once you get your hands on a plane, use it for long trips, especially if you want to uncover the map. And keep spare clothes in the trunk, because they help with every getaway.
