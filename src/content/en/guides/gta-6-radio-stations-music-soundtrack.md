GTA without the radio wouldn't be GTA. In October, Rockstar revealed the first six stations for GTA 6, with hosts including Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and two founding members of Slayer. Today the reviews add more: there are far more stations, and for the first time in the series there are podcasts. Here's what's official, what the reviews report and what so far only comes from leaks.

**As of November 18, 2026, 5 p.m. CET.** The full station list will only be known once the game is out. After launch, we'll check every station ourselves and update this guide.

## The short answer

- **Official:** six stations, revealed on October 8 on Rockstar's music page, each with a preview clip.
- **Per the reviews:** more than 20 stations and several podcasts with their own episodes.
- **Everywhere:** in cars, in many buildings and on foot with headphones.
- **Separate from that:** GTA VI: The Album with 34 songs on November 19, and Justice's score on December 11.

## The six official stations

In the game, the hosts go by on-air names. Who's behind them was reported by outlets such as KeenGamer at the reveal. The songs come from Rockstar's preview clips.

| Station | Music | Hosts | Songs from the preview |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cocoteo FM | Latin: reggaeton, bachata, merengue, dembow | El Martillo and La Gata (Bad Bunny and RaiNao) | Bad Bunny "EoO", Romeo Santos "Canalla" |
| Back Country Radio | renegade country | MW and Delta Dawne (Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey) | David Allan Coe, Loretta Lynn |
| AfroBank FM | African classics, amapiano, 3-step | Burna and Palmsy (Burna Boy and Palms Trax) | Asake "Joha", Pat Thomas with Ebo Taylor |
| The Chamber 106.6 | metal from the '80s onward | DJ Kerry and DJ Tom (Kerry King and Tom Araya) | Black Sabbath, Pantera, Slayer "Raining Blood" |
| Flash FM | pop and dance | Robyn and Alex (Robyn and Alex Consani) | Charli xcx "Good Ones", MGMT "Electric Feel" |
| Dirty South Classics | Southern rap and hip-hop classics | Trick and Trina (Trick Daddy and Trina) | David Banner, DJ Khaled with Trick Daddy, Rick Ross and Pitbull |

Back Country Radio also features Mud Dog Mike, a swamp meteorologist who reports from an airboat, according to Game Informer.

## Flash FM: the nod to Vice City

Of the six, Flash FM is the old friend: it already played in GTA Vice City back in 2002. Rockstar's music page calls it a Vice City institution. Whether other stations from back then return, Rockstar hasn't confirmed.

## How many stations are there in total?

According to Shacknews, GTA 6 has more than 20 radio stations. Alongside the six revealed stations, Rockstar's music page explicitly mentions classic rock, talk radio and podcasts, without naming them.

Lists with more station names are circulating online, for example at Insider Gaming, including Vice City FM, V-Rock, Symphony FM and Honey FM. Insider Gaming says these names come from marketing material and leaks and could still change. None of them are confirmed. We'll check the list in the game after launch.

## Podcasts: a first for GTA

Rockstar says GTA 6 is the first game in the series with on-demand podcasts. Here's how they work:

- **In the car and on foot:** Rockstar says radio and podcasts play in vehicles and while you're out walking.
- **Headphones:** On foot, you need new headphones or earbuds that connect to your phone.
- **Multiple episodes:** Shacknews reports several podcasts, each with multiple episodes.
- **True crime:** Kotaku describes a true crime podcast hosted by two terrible people, and GamingTrend mentions murder mystery podcasts.
- **Radios everywhere:** Shacknews says most buildings and rooms have a radio, and headphones also work during gym workouts.

## Ads and talk

Commercials are part of the GTA radio experience. According to the previews, GTA 6 has the largest collection of radio and TV ads in the series' history. Examples shown include spots for the antidepressant Angstipan and the 30,000-calorie Prairie Sandwich.

## What the reviews say about the music

- **Kotaku:** calls the licensed soundtrack across the stations incredible, and describes Justice's score as a wild mix of electronic dance, '80s funk and rock guitars.
- **TheGamer:** calls it the best radio selection the series has had since Vice City, and says Justice's music lands the big story moments perfectly.
- **GamingTrend:** finds something for everyone but notes a heavy lean toward Latin music, with some stations entirely in Spanish. The reviewer would have liked a Spotify tie-in.
- **Shacknews:** calls the radio overhauled and improved, and one of the biggest sources of immersion.

## Radio, album and score: the difference

| What | Content | Release |
|---|---|---|
| In-game radio | stations, hosts, ads, podcasts | with the game |
| GTA VI: The Album | 34 new songs, including tracks by Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen, Cardi B and Keith Richards | November 19 |
| GTA VI: Original Score | 30 tracks by Justice plus digital bonus tracks | December 11 |

A leaked tracklist for the album has been circulating since November 14. It isn't confirmed. More on that in our [album news](/en/news/2026-11-14-gta-6-album-release-tracklist-leak/).

## Our tip

Listen to the six stations on Rockstar's music page ahead of time, so you know where to tune in at midnight. On foot, grab headphones as soon as you can. And if you come across a leaked station list, treat it as speculation until launch. We'll add the real list once we've checked it in the game.
