GTA 6 is built around a duo: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Rockstar sums them up on the official game page: "Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them." Here's everything that's confirmed about the two and their circle, without story spoilers beyond what the trailers and previews have shown.

**As of November 18, 2026**

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia grew up in Liberty City and wants the good life her mom always dreamed of. She's a trained fighter. Fighting for her family landed her in the **Leonida Penitentiary**. As Rockstar puts it: "Life has been coming at her swinging ever since."

Trailer 2 opens with Lucia leaving prison together with Jason. In Vice City, she's ready to change the odds in her favor.

**What the previews reveal:** According to preview reports, Lucia's **ankle monitor** plays a central role in the campaign and kicks off her partnership with Jason. In the launch trailer, Eurogamer spotted what may be an ankle monitor on Lucia's leg and reads it as a hint that she's on parole. Rockstar hasn't confirmed that interpretation.

## Jason Duval

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. He tried to take a different path through the Army, which didn't work out. Today he's back in the Keys, working for local drug runners. Rockstar's short version: "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder."

He gets to live rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties. In return, he helps with shakedowns and drops by for sangria with Brian and his third wife, Lori.

Early in the launch trailer, Jason receives a reverse-charge call. Eurogamer reads it as a possible call from Lucia in jail. Again, that's an interpretation, not a confirmation.

## The duo in the game

Rockstar describes the story as a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida. The Extended Look and the previews show how Jason and Lucia play:

- **Switching:** When they're together, you switch instantly, for example from the driver's seat to the passenger seat. When they're apart, switching takes a few seconds, similar to GTA 5. During some story moments, it's not possible at all.
- **A life of their own:** The character you're not controlling follows their own schedule and sends you texts. Ignoring them all the time can hurt the relationship.
- **Robbing together:** Robberies can be done solo or as a pair. Joint jobs strengthen the bond.
- **Caught separately:** One can be arrested while the other escapes.
- **Optional romance:** The two start out with a romantic interest. Whether it goes further depends on your decisions and the time you spend together. The romance is roleplay and gives no gameplay advantages.
- **Shared home base:** Jason and Lucia share a home. Much like the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2, other characters come and go there.

## The supporting cast

Alongside Trailer 2 in May 2025, Rockstar introduced six more characters:

| Character | Who are they? |
|---|---|
| Cal Hampton | Jason's buddy. Listens in on Coast Guard comms over a beer and browses in incognito tabs. According to Rockstar, "Cal is at the low tide of America and happy there." One of Brian Heder's associates. |
| Brian Heder | A classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys. According to Rockstar, he's been around long enough to let others do his dirty work. |
| Boobie Ike | A Vice City legend who made it to the top. His legit empire spans real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. |
| Dre'Quan Priest | Dealt on the streets and wanted to make it big in music. Rockstar calls him "more of a hustler than a gangster." Now signed to a label. |
| Real Dimez | The rap duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy. Built a big social media following after an early hit with rapper DWNPLY and are signed to Only Raw Records. |
| Raul Bautista | A seasoned bank robber, always on the hunt for people willing to take big risks for big rewards. Known as reckless and likes to raise the stakes. |

Rockstar hasn't revealed how big each character's role is. Insider Gaming speculates that Cal could play a role for the duo similar to Lester in GTA 5. That's a guess.

## Our take

Jason and Lucia feel less like two separate stories that cross paths and more like a real team. The switching system, the texts and the joint robberies show that Rockstar is making the relationship the core of the game. We find it especially interesting that the romance is optional. That gives you real control over the dynamic between the two.

What stands out about the supporting cast is how heavily Rockstar leans on music, social media and Vice City's club scene. Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan and Real Dimez are all tied to that business. If you enjoy the series' satire, there should be plenty of material here.
