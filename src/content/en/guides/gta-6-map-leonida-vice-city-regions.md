GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. Vice City sits at its center, surrounded by islands, swamps, small towns and a national park. Here are all six regions Rockstar has introduced, with the official descriptions, known locations and our take.

**As of November 18, 2026.** We'll expand this guide once we've explored the map ourselves.

## Leonida at a glance

| Region | Type | In short |
|---|---|---|
| Vice City | Metropolis | Beaches, Art Deco hotels, nightlife, a cruise port |
| Leonida Keys | Island chain | Tropical, laid-back bars, dangerous waters |
| Grassrivers | Wetlands | Mangroves, alligators and worse |
| Port Gellhorn | Declining coastal town | Cheap motels, empty strip malls |
| Ambrosia | Working-class town | A sugar refinery and a biker gang |
| Mount Kalaga National Park | Wilderness | Hunting, fishing, off-road trails, recluses |

Rockstar introduced the regions on its official website in May 2025, alongside Trailer 2.

## Vice City

Rockstar sums up the city in one line on the official game page: Vice City, USA, "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America."

The city is modeled on Miami. According to Rockstar, each neighborhood has something to offer, from the pastel Art Deco hotels to the bright white sands of **Ocean Beach**. According to Insider Gaming, you can also expect:

- beachfront hotels
- a neighborhood in the style of Little Havana
- a flea market
- a major cruise port

The previews tell us that Vice City is a beast in gameplay terms too: crowded, narrow streets and heavy traffic make high-star chases and vehicle deliveries noticeably harder.

More place names appear in the descriptions of the Ultimate Edition: a boat is docked at **Washington Beach**, a garage sits in **Watson Bay**. What the Ultimate Edition is all about is covered in our [editions comparison](/en/guides/gta-6-editions-standard-vs-ultimate/).

## Leonida Keys

A tropical island chain in the south with laid-back bars, though its waters can turn dangerous. The Keys are central to the story: Jason works for local drug runners there and lives rent-free in a property owned by Brian Heder, an old smuggler from the golden age of the Keys.

The Keys also play a big role in the launch trailer. The very first shot shows an area that earlier trailers barely touched. In our view, the Florida Keys inspiration is hard to miss.

## Grassrivers

Rockstar calls Grassrivers the "untamable jewel" of Leonida. A vast wetland full of mangroves where alligators are the least of your worries. According to Rockstar, far deadlier predators and weirder discoveries await among the mangroves.

Our take: that sounds a lot like the Everglades, and like a region that rewards exploration.

## Port Gellhorn

A town that has seen better days: cheap motels, empty strip malls, closed attractions. What's left of the economy is mostly illicit, according to Insider Gaming. Rockstar's own description is one of the sharpest in the game: Port Gellhorn is "fueled by malt liquor, painkillers, and truck stop energy drinks."

## Ambrosia

A working-class town in the heart of Leonida. Most jobs depend on the **Allied Crystal** sugar refinery, and a biker gang appears to run the town. If you're into small towns, industry and trouble with bikers, this is probably your place.

## Mount Kalaga National Park

The national park lies on Leonida's northern border. According to Rockstar, it offers hunting, fishing and off-road trails. It's also home to residents who want nothing to do with the government. Rockstar describes them as "hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals."

## How big is the map really?

Caution is warranted here. Rockstar has described the map as very big but has **not given a number**. What's circulating online are estimates from the previews:

| Comparison | Estimate from previews |
|---|---|
| Playable area vs. GTA 5 | About twice as big |
| Landmass vs. Red Dead Redemption 2 | About three times as big |
| Vice City vs. Los Santos | About twice as big |

None of these figures is official. But they point in a clear direction: if the press impressions are right, Leonida will be considerably bigger than GTA 5's Los Santos and Blaine County.

## What you can do in Leonida

According to the previews, the world offers plenty to do away from missions:

- Pool, mini golf and a zoo
- Kayaking, scuba diving and parachuting
- Working out
- A wildlife checklist for animals you observe and record

More on gameplay systems, vehicles and getting around is in our [tips for your first hours](/en/guides/gta-6-first-hours-tips-gameplay-systems/).

## Our take

Leonida looks like Rockstar's most varied map yet: a big city with real crowds, plus islands, swamp, small-town gloom and wilderness. We're especially excited about the regions beyond Vice City. Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia sound like exactly the kind of offbeat stories the series is known for. How well it all fits together, we'll find out tonight.
