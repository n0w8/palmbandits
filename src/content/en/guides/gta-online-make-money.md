Want to make money in GTA Online without grinding in circles for hours? Then you're in the right place. We'll show you which methods are still worth it in November 2026, what you should buy first and how to cash in on the weekly bonuses.

**As of November 17, 2026.** Rockstar adjusts payouts regularly. All figures are rough estimates without event bonuses.

## The essentials first

In July 2026, Rockstar cut many heist payouts with the Kortz Center Heist update. At the same time, for many heists the **first completion each week** pays significantly more than any repeat. The week always starts on Thursday.

That gives you the best strategy for 2026:

1. Check the new event bonuses every Thursday.
2. Play the jobs with a weekly bonus once.
3. Then play the best repeatable job.
4. Keep passive businesses running on the side.

## No starting cash: your first millions

### Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid

You don't need any property. You start the mission series through Vincent Effenburger. The first completion each week pays more, and repeats are a bit weaker but still good for getting started.

### Contact Missions with an event bonus

Missions from Lamar, Gerald, Simeon or Martin Madrazo normally pay very little. With a double or quadruple bonus, though, they turn into a relaxed source of money. Right now, Contact Missions even pay 2x until December 2, or 4x with GTA+.

### Weekly Challenges

Almost every week there's a challenge that pays a fixed amount, often GTA$100,000 or more. It usually only costs you a few rounds.

## The best investments

### Kosatka and Cayo Perico

The Kosatka is still one of the best first purchases. The Cayo Perico Heist can be done completely solo and is easy to plan.

- Since the July update, primary targets range from around GTA$400,000 (Tequila) to GTA$910,000 (Pink Diamond).
- On top of that comes secondary loot like gold, art or cocaine, which you grab yourself.
- Solo, you have to wait about 2.4 hours after the finale before you can start it again. Use that time for other jobs.

### Agency and the Dr. Dre Contract

The Agency unlocks the contract "The Data Leaks" with Dr. Dre. You need to complete a Security Contract first. The finale pays around GTA$900,000 per replay, a bit more on the first completion of the week. The Agency also has a safe that fills up passively.

### Auto Shop and Salvage Yard

Both are good additions for short sessions. Auto Shop contracts are quick, and the Salvage Yard brings in solid sums with its robberies and by breaking down vehicles. But only buy them once you own the Kosatka and the Agency.

### Kortz Center Heist (endgame)

The newest major heist requires a Mansion plus the Art Studio. That's a huge investment. It's only worth it if you're already well in the black and play the heist once every week for the bonus.

## Passive income: money in the background

Important up front: **No business earns money while you're offline.** Production only happens while you're in a session. That still makes passive businesses a perfect bonus while you play heists.

| Business | Why it's worth it |
|---|---|
| Acid Lab | Cheap entry through the "The First Dose" mission series, runs well solo |
| Nightclub | Collects goods from your other businesses, plus a safe that fills up |
| Bunker | Solid production, good sell missions |
| Cocaine Lockup (MC) | The strongest classic MC business |
| Money Fronts | Hands On Car Wash, Smoke on the Water and Higgins Helitours add extra background income |

**Tip:** If you're short on time, buy supplies instead of stealing them. It saves trips and nerves.

## GTA+: is it worth it?

GTA+ costs real money, but gives you GTA$500,000 every month, stacked bonuses on selected activities and free vehicles. If you play a lot and would pay anyway, you'll get something out of it. It's not a must.

## Our order for beginners

1. **Play the Farm Raid and Contact Missions** with event bonuses.
2. **Buy the Kosatka** and farm Cayo Perico solo.
3. **Buy the Agency** and unlock the Dr. Dre Contract.
4. **Acid Lab and Nightclub** for passive income.
5. **Bunker, MC businesses, Auto Shop, Salvage Yard** depending on your interests and bank balance.
6. **Mansion and Kortz Center** once your bank account is really full.
