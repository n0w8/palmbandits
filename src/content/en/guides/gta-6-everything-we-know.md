GTA 6 is here, almost: Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here you'll find everything Rockstar has officially confirmed about the release, story, map and characters, clearly separated from rumors and speculation.

**As of November 17, 2026.** We'll update this page after launch.

## Leonida: the setting

GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of **Leonida**, Rockstar's take on Florida. At its heart lies **Vice City**, the Miami stand-in fans already know from 2002's GTA Vice City. According to Rockstar, the game explores the dark side of the sunniest place in America.

### The six regions

So far, Rockstar has revealed these six regions:

| Region | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Vice City | The metropolis: beaches, nightlife, glamour and plenty of crime |
| Leonida Keys | Tropical island chain in the south, inspired by the Florida Keys |
| Grassrivers | Vast swamp and wetlands modeled on the Everglades |
| Port Gellhorn | Run-down coastal town that has seen better days |
| Ambrosia | Industry and farming, dominated by a huge sugar refinery |
| Mount Kalaga National Park | National park with nature and wilderness for outdoor fans |

Rockstar hasn't revealed the exact map size in numbers. Any figures you see online are estimates.

## The main characters

### Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the first female protagonist of the modern GTA era. She has already done time in prison and wants a better life for herself and her family. The very first trailer opens with her behind bars.

### Jason Duval

Jason grew up surrounded by grifters and crooks and served in the military. In the Leonida Keys, he works for local drug runners and really just wants a quiet life. As you can probably guess, that doesn't work out.

### The duo

After a supposedly simple job goes wrong, the two get caught up in a conspiracy that stretches across all of Leonida. The trailers lean heavily into Bonnie and Clyde vibes.

### Other known characters

Rockstar has already introduced several supporting characters:

- **Cal Hampton:** Jason's buddy
- **Brian Heder:** veteran smuggler in the Keys
- **Boobie Ike:** Vice City heavyweight with businesses in clubs and music
- **Dre'Quan Priest:** wants to make it big in the music business
- **Real Dimez:** a rap duo
- **Raul Bautista:** seasoned bank robber

## Editions and bonus

- **Standard Edition:** €79.99
- **Ultimate Edition:** €99.99, with extra vehicles, weapons and clothing
- **Vintage Vice City Pack:** bonus for all pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026

## Tech

- The target is **30 FPS** on all consoles, including the PS5 Pro. The Pro gets visual enhancements.
- A 60 FPS mode has not been announced.
- The download needs up to **212 GB** of free storage.

## What the trailers reveal

So far, the trailers are the best source for how the game looks:

- **Trailer 1 (December 2023):** Opens with Lucia in prison, shows Vice City by day and night and plenty of clips styled like viral social media videos. Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" saw a huge streaming boom afterward.
- **Trailer 2 (May 2025):** More story, more Jason, more Leonida. Rockstar also introduced the regions and supporting characters on its official website.
- **Launch trailer (October 15, 2026):** Confirmed the November 19 date.

Of course, what you see in trailers is staged. Only the launch will show how the game actually plays.

## Rumors and speculation

Here we draw a clear line: none of this has been confirmed by Rockstar.

- **PC release date:** Many expect the PC version to follow a year or more later again. That's pure speculation based on GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2.
- **GTA 6 Online:** Almost everyone expects a new online mode, but Rockstar hasn't shared any details or dates. Numbers such as players per lobby come from leaks, and dates like 2027 come from third-party estimates.
- **Map size:** The comparisons with GTA 5 doing the rounds are estimates from fans and dataminers.
- **Physical disc:** Reports of a later disc version are unconfirmed.
