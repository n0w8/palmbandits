GTA Online glitches promise millions in minutes. Sounds great, but it can cost you all of your progress. In this guide you'll learn why money and dupe glitches are so risky, what Rockstar typically does about them and how to make money safely instead.

**As of November 17, 2026.** We deliberately don't give any instructions for exploits here.

## What even is a glitch?

Not every bug is the same. Roughly speaking, there are three groups:

| Type | Example | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Harmless bug | Car stuck in a wall | None |
| Gameplay shortcut | Completing a mission in an unexpected way | Low |
| Exploit | Duplicating money, cars or items, collecting payouts multiple times | High |

**Rule of thumb:** If a trick creates money or items out of nothing or triggers a payout multiple times, it's an exploit. And that's exactly where Rockstar stops joking around.

## What Rockstar typically does about exploits

Rockstar doesn't always react right away, but it's often thorough. The usual measures:

1. **Hotfix:** The glitch is closed server-side or via an update, often within a few days.
2. **Money removal:** Ill-gotten GTA$ is removed from your account, often along with everything you bought with it.
3. **Character reset:** Progress, properties and vehicles are gone.
4. **Ban:** temporary or permanent, depending on severity and repeat offenses.

On top of that, penalties often come **in waves and with a delay**. Just because nothing happens for a week after a glitch doesn't mean you're safe. Rockstar analyzes transactions on its own servers and can react weeks later.

## Real cases from the past

No details on the methods, just so you get a feel for the consequences:

- **Casino glitches:** Players who duplicated chips through a bug had the ill-gotten money taken away again. Repeat offenders risked bans.
- **Duplication exploits on the new consoles:** A dupe glitch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S ended in permanent bans for those involved.
- **Full account resets:** Rockstar has also completely reset the accounts of exploit users in the past.

## How to spot a risky trick

Many exploit videos look harmless. These are the warning signs you should know:

- **"Infinite money" or "infinite cars":** Anything that duplicates value is an exploit.
- **Deliberately disconnecting:** If a trick requires you to cut your internet at the right moment or manipulate the session, skip it.
- **Time pressure:** "Quick, before it gets patched" really means: Rockstar already knows.
- **Third-party software or devices:** As soon as extra software is involved, you're heading into mod menu territory.
- **Second account required:** Tricks that shuffle money or vehicles back and forth between accounts stand out in particular.

## Fake generators and phishing

There are sites online promising "free GTA$" or "Shark Card generators". Such things don't exist. These sites want your login details for your Rockstar account, PSN or Xbox, or they make you click through surveys and downloads. At best, you only lose time, at worst your account. Only enter your login details on the official Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft websites, and turn on two-factor authentication.

## Mod menus and "recovery" offers

Bought shortcuts are even more dangerous than glitches:

- **Mod menus:** On PC, Rockstar has been using the BattlEye anti-cheat software since 2024. As a rule, never launch GTA Online with mods installed.
- **"Recovery" services:** Strangers want your login details to load money or levels onto your account. That breaks the rules, and you're handing your account to someone you don't know. Account theft included.
- **Gifted money drops:** If a modder makes money bags rain down in your session, don't pick anything up and switch sessions.

## If you did get caught

- Contact **official Rockstar Support** only.
- Never give your login details to so-called "unban services". That's a scam.
- Be honest with yourself: if you glitched, your odds aren't good.

## Safe alternatives that actually make money

The good news: there's more to earn legitimately in GTA Online than many people think.

- **Weekly bonuses:** The events change every Thursday. Double or triple payouts are often worth more than any glitch, with zero risk. Right now, for example, Freemode Challenges pay 4x and Stunt Races 3x until December 2.
- **First completion each week:** Many heists pay significantly more the first time you finish them in a given week.
- **Solo heists and contracts:** Cayo Perico and the Dr. Dre Contract remain reliable sources of money.
- **Passive businesses:** Nightclub, Acid Lab and co. run in the background.

## Story Mode: cheating is allowed here

In GTA 5 Story Mode, cheats are explicitly allowed, whether you use codes, phone numbers or button combinations. The only catch: achievements and trophies are disabled for the current session. These cheats don't work in GTA Online.
