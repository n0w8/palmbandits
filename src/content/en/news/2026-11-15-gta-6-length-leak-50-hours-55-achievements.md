How long does GTA 6 take to beat? According to an Insider Gaming report from November 15, the main story runs about 50 hours across almost 100 missions. Hitting 100 percent is said to take around 200 hours. On top of that, a leak claims GTA 6 launches with 55 achievements. Rockstar hasn't confirmed any of these numbers.

**Rumor alert:** Every number in this story comes from reports and leaks, not from Rockstar. **As of November 15, 2026.**

## What the report claims

Insider Gaming says it spoke with people who have already played the game. That fits with reports that review code has been in media hands since late October. The key claims:

| Claim | Value | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Main story without side content | about 50 hours | according to Insider Gaming |
| Number of missions | close to 100 | GTA 5 had around 70 |
| 100 percent completion | about 200 hours | explicitly just an estimate |

Other sites such as Noobfeed go further: five chapters, around 170 animal species and an achievement called "Zoologist" that requires studying every animal. They don't say where those details come from. Even more caution is warranted here than with the playtime.

## What Rockstar itself has said

The only real number from Rockstar is indirect. Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, told The New York Times that his last full playthrough in February took around 80 hours. That included the main story and optional goals with narrative consequences.

So the numbers don't necessarily contradict each other. Fifty hours for the story alone and 80 hours with some optional goals can fit together nicely. If you take your time, explore the world and do activities along the way, you'll likely need a lot longer.

## 55 achievements and a 1,000-point mystery

On November 8, Twisted Voxel reported on a GTAForums post claiming GTA 6 launches on Xbox with 55 achievements. According to the user, 54 of them are worth between 1 and 54 Gamerscore each. The 55th is supposedly worth 1,000 Gamerscore on its own.

What's behind it is unclear. Two explanations are being discussed:

- a so-called Mythic achievement on Xbox
- a placeholder for "unlock all achievements"

GTABase also lists 55 entries and cites data from the Xbox backend as its source. On PS5, that would mean 54 trophies plus a Platinum. Since GTA 6 has no online mode at launch, every achievement would be doable in single-player. In GTA 5, some achievements were tied to GTA Online.

## Our take

Fifty hours sounds plausible, especially since Rob Nelson's 80 hours including optional goals set a frame of reference. For comparison: according to HowLongToBeat, players need about 90 hours on average for 100 percent in GTA 5, while completionists spend close to 200 hours in Red Dead Redemption 2. An estimated 200 hours for everything in GTA 6 would put it on RDR2's level.

The 55 achievements rest on thinner ground: a forum post and backend data that can still change before launch. There's no reliable source yet for achievement names or descriptions.

Reviews and, at the latest, the November 19 launch will bring clarity. That's when the achievement and trophy lists on PS5 and Xbox become publicly visible.
