When preloads opened on November 10, Rockstar also confirmed GTA 6's key technical details. The game targets 30 frames per second on every console, including the PS5 Pro. In return, the Pro gets a sharper image via PSSR and exclusive ray-traced shadows. Xbox players can also stream GTA 6 via the cloud at launch, even on an Xbox One.

**As of November 10, 2026**

## The key specs by platform

| Platform | Frame rate | Extras according to Rockstar |
|---|---|---|
| PS5 | 30 FPS target | DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D Audio, faster loading |
| PS5 Pro | 30 FPS target | plus PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows |
| Xbox Series X | 30 FPS target | no specific extras named |
| Xbox Series S | 30 FPS target | no specific extras named |
| Xbox Cloud Gaming | Series S settings | also playable on Xbox One |

Rockstar says Remote Play is supported on both PlayStation and Xbox. It hasn't named a resolution for any console.

## 30 FPS: a deliberate choice

Rockstar describes its approach as pushing both console families to their absolute limits, with visual fidelity taking priority over frame rate. That's no surprise: right after the big gameplay presentation in August, Digital Foundry explained that 60 FPS on consoles was extremely unlikely. According to the analysts, it would require freeing up a lot more processing time, especially on the CPU, and the PS5 Pro's CPU is barely stronger than the base PS5's.

Many fans are hoping for a 40 FPS mode for 120 Hz TVs. Rockstar hasn't announced anything like that. Whether a performance mode arrives later remains open.

## PS5 Pro: sharper, not smoother

If you own a PS5 Pro, Rockstar says you get two extras:

- **PSSR:** Sony's AI upscaling reconstructs a sharper image from a lower internal resolution for extra clarity. Rockstar didn't say which PSSR version is used.
- **Ray-traced shadows:** These are exclusive to the PS5 Pro.

You don't get more frames per second on the Pro. Rockstar also says GTA 6 plays best on PlayStation 5, which reflects the marketing partnership with Sony that has been running since the summer.

## DualSense and 3D audio

On every PS5 model, GTA 6 uses the DualSense controller's haptics. The intensity can be adjusted in the accessibility settings. On top of that you get Tempest 3D Audio for spatial sound and shorter load times thanks to the SSD.

## Xbox Cloud Gaming and Xbox One

For Xbox players, the cloud option is the real news. GTA 6 is playable via Xbox Cloud Gaming at launch, streamed with Series S settings. According to Rockstar, that means you can even play on an Xbox One, although there's no native version for the older console. Rockstar notes that connection speed and other factors affect performance. The reports don't say which subscription tier you need.

## Our take

For most players, little changes: 30 FPS has been expected for months. What matters is how stable those 30 FPS are in practice, especially in dense districts and during chases. Only reviews and the first tech analyses after launch will tell.

Based on the confirmed details, the PS5 Pro is mainly worth it for a cleaner, sharper image. If you're thinking of upgrading just for GTA 6, wait for the post-launch tech comparisons. The cloud option is interesting for anyone still on an Xbox One, but only with a stable, fast internet connection.
