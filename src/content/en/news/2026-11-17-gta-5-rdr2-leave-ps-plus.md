As of Tuesday, November 17, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 are no longer part of the PS Plus Extra and Premium game catalog. If you only played these two Rockstar games through the subscription, you're locked out unless you buy them. The timing stands out: the departure comes exactly two days before GTA 6 launches.

**As of November 17, 2026**

## These eleven games have left PS Plus

Sony confirmed the list on October 20. Affected titles:

| Game | Platform |
|---|---|
| Grand Theft Auto V | PS5, PS4 |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | PS4 |
| Football Manager 26 | PS5 |
| The Talos Principle 2 | PS5 |
| Cricket 24 | PS5, PS4 |
| Monster Boy and the Cursed Kingdom | PS5, PS4 |
| Hotshot Racing | PS4 |
| Rock of Ages 3: Make & Break | PS4 |
| Earth Defense Force 4.1: The Shadow of New Despair | PS4 |
| Rapala Fishing Pro Series | PS4 |
| Yet Another Zombie Defense HD | PS4 |

Red Dead Redemption 2 was only included as the PS4 version, but it also runs on PS5 via backward compatibility.

## What happens to your save?

Unlike the monthly games on PS Plus Essential, Extra and Premium titles lose access as soon as a game leaves the catalog. That applies even if you had downloaded it.

Sony warned PS5 users back in October with a notice in the main menu. According to reports, the message suggested three options: finish the story in time, buy the game at a discount to keep your progress, or pre-order GTA VI. So if you buy GTA 5 now, you can carry on with your existing save.

## What about GTA Online?

If you played GTA Online through the PS5 version of GTA 5 from the subscription, you now also need your own copy to keep playing. On PS5, GTA Online can also be bought on its own. Playing online on PlayStation still requires a PS Plus subscription on top, and the Essential tier is enough for that.

The current bonuses on Freemode Challenges and Contact Missions run until December 2 according to Rockstar, so beyond the GTA 6 launch. Details are in our story [GTA Online: 4x GTA$ and RP on Freemode Challenges Until December 2](/en/news/2026-11-15-gta-online-event-4x-freemode-challenges/).

## Why now?

Sony hasn't officially explained the timing. Games leave PS Plus regularly, usually because licensing deals expire. GTA 5 had only returned to the catalog in November 2025 after a short break.

Our take: a date two days before GTA 6 is hardly a coincidence. The nudge in Sony's own message to pre-order GTA VI makes the direction pretty clear. If you wanted one last spin around Los Santos during the week of the biggest launch in years, you're meant to either buy in or move straight on to Leonida.

Whether GTA 5 and RDR2 will return at some point is open. Push Square thinks a return to the catalog is likely, but nothing is confirmed.

## What you can do now

1. **Haven't finished the story?** Check the PlayStation Store. GTA 5 has been discounted regularly for years.
2. **Just waiting for GTA 6?** Then you don't need GTA 5. GTA 6 is a standalone game and doesn't require any prior knowledge.
3. **Want to keep playing GTA Online?** Buy GTA 5 or the standalone GTA Online version for PS5. Rockstar manages your character through your account.
