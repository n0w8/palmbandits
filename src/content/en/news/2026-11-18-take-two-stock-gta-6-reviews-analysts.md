GTA 6's reviews are moving more than just players. Shares in Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, rose sharply after the game landed a Metascore of 99. At the same time, several analysts restated their forecasts, and they are huge: the highest estimate on Wall Street is 45 million copies sold by the end of December.

**As of: November 18, 2026, 4:56 pm CET (15:56 GMT).** Prices change constantly. This article is not investment advice.

## How the stock reacted

- **Pre-market:** According to Investing.com, the shares rose about 6.4 percent on Wednesday morning.
- **During trading:** Benzinga reported a gain of just under 5 percent at one point, to $213.82.
- **The run-up:** In the week before the embargo, the stock had fallen about 12 percent, according to Investing.com.

The trigger is obvious: at 99, GTA 6 sits level with Zelda: Ocarina of Time at the top of Metacritic. Benzinga also points to Jason Schreier, who played for more than 45 hours for Bloomberg Businessweek and essentially called the game a masterpiece.

## What analysts expect

| Analyst | Rating | Price target | Forecast |
|---|---|---|---|
| BMO Capital | Outperform | $285 | 45 million units in the October to December quarter |
| Oppenheimer (Martin Yang) | Outperform | $280 | around 20 million units in the first three days |
| B. Riley | Buy | $300 | no unit figure given |
| DA Davidson | Buy | $300 | no unit figure given |
| TD Cowen | Buy | $284 | no unit figure given |

Investing.com says BMO's 45 million is the highest estimate on the Street, against a consensus of 34 million. Oppenheimer expects more than $1.85 billion in gross revenue over the first three days. The most bullish voices, per Investing.com, even talk about 20 to 25 million copies on day one.

For comparison, Oppenheimer cites market research firm Sensor Tower, which puts pre-orders at 15.88 million units. That number is not official.

## When real numbers arrive

Every sales figure so far is an estimate. According to Oppenheimer, Take-Two could share a first official number as early as **November 20**, with an update on the opening weekend possibly around **November 23**. Investing.com flags a press release on the first 24 hours as the next big moment for the stock.

Take-Two most recently reaffirmed its full-year outlook of $8.0 to $8.2 billion in net bookings for the fiscal year ending March 2027. A large share of that depends on GTA 6.

## One forecast is already out of date

Oppenheimer itself shows how hard this is to call. The firm expected a Twitch peak of around 2 million viewers. By this afternoon, preliminary Streams Charts data already showed more than 3 million, before Europe and the Americas had even unlocked.

## Our take

For Take-Two, the reviews were the last big unknown before launch. That is gone now. From here on it is all about one question: how many copies sell in the first few days? The range of estimates is so wide that even a record launch could disappoint some expectations.

As a player, the stock market is a side story. One point is still worth noting: the better GTA 6 sells, the more room Rockstar has for updates, expansions and a new online mode. Whether and when that comes is still open.
