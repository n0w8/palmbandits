For some companies, the GTA 6 release on November 19 is turning into a semi-holiday. Logitech is giving its roughly 7,000 employees a paid day to play games, and the first one falls right on launch day. Palworld developer Pocketpair is sending its team, tongue firmly in cheek, on a five-day business trip to Vice City. And according to recent data, time-off requests for launch day are clearly above normal.

**As of November 14, 2026**

## Logitech: one paid gaming day per year

Logitech introduced its "Logi Game Day" on November 10. According to Notebookcheck and GGRecon:

- **Who:** around 7,000 employees worldwide
- **What:** one extra paid day off per year to play a game of their choice
- **When:** The first Game Day is November 19. Anyone who can't take it that day can use it any time until November 2027.
- **Offices:** stay open on November 19

Logitech says it's the first company-wide gaming day in its history. Its gaming brand Logitech G also plans a livestream from its San Jose headquarters on launch day. CEO Hanneke Faber told Fortune, in essence, that it's time to drop the stereotype of gaming as a waste of time. Whether the day becomes a permanent annual perk is still open.

## Pocketpair: a five-day business trip to Vice City

On November 12, Pocketpair, the studio behind Palworld, posted a tongue-in-cheek memo on company letterhead on X. In it, employees are granted special leave for a supposedly urgent business trip to Vice City.

- **Dates:** November 19 to 23
- **Actual leave days:** three, since a weekend falls in between
- **Work on its own games:** According to Pocketpair, updates for Palworld and Craftopia are still on schedule

Reports differ on whether the whole team is off or just everyone who wants to play GTA 6.

## A tuning shop closes completely

Back in the summer, Burger Motorsports, a tuning specialist from California, made headlines. After several employees requested November 19 off, the company decided to close entirely for the day.

## Time-off requests well above normal

Data from HR software provider BambooHR shows these aren't isolated cases. According to CNBC, time-off requests for November 19 are almost 13 percent higher than on comparable days in 2024 and 2025. Other reports put the figure between 9 and 17 percent depending on the comparison. Nearly one in five workers plans to take time off for the launch. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick had predicted earlier in the year that plenty of people would call in sick on release day.

## Our take

Of course this is good marketing too. Logitech sells gaming gear, Pocketpair makes games itself, and both get plenty of attention on a date when everybody is talking about games anyway. Still, it shows how big the GTA 6 launch is: a single game measurably shifts the vacation plans of a lot of workers.

What it means for you in practice:

1. **Sort out your time off early:** If you want November 19 or 20 off and haven't asked yet, do it now.
2. **Don't fake a sick day:** A false sick note can have consequences at work. A regular day off is the better choice.
3. **Plan for the night:** The game unlocks at midnight local time according to store listings. If you start playing overnight, you probably don't want an 8 AM meeting the next morning.
