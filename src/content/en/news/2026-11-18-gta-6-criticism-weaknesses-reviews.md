A Metascore of 99 does not mean GTA 6 has no weaknesses. According to Kotaku, the lowest score on Metacritic is a 9 out of 10, and even the perfect scores mention things that might bug you. We went through the reviews looking for concrete criticism. The upshot: nothing is a deal-breaker, but there are a few things worth knowing before you start.

**As of: November 18, 2026, 4:55 pm CET (15:55 GMT).**

## What gets criticised, and how often

Output Lag went through 18 reviews and counted how often each complaint came up:

| Criticism | Reviews |
|---|---|
| Frame rate dips below 30 FPS | 9 of 18 |
| Pacing: slow opening, downtime between jobs, weaker back half | 8 of 18 |
| Specific issues with story or characters | 8 of 18 |
| Realism takes getting used to: fragile cars, repair bills, daily chores | 6 of 18 |
| Police and Heat system fussy early on | 4 of 18 |
| Stiff melee combat | 2 of 18 |
| Satire too tame or crude jokes falling flat | 2 of 18 |

Worth keeping in mind: despite those points, 16 of the 18 reviews praise the story overall.

## Controls and menus

This is where reviewers get most specific:

- **Cover:** GamePro calls the cover system a bit fiddly, saying it does not always register reliably.
- **Radial menus:** At first they feel nested and confusing, according to GamePro.
- **Too many actions:** TheGamer finds the controls slightly overcomplicated because of the sheer number of interactions and needed a few hours to adjust. There is a classic control option, though.
- **Movement:** PC Games says Jason and Lucia still occasionally snag on edges and objects, if less often than Arthur did in Red Dead Redemption 2. In tight spaces the controls feel imprecise.

## Missions and pacing

GamePro describes the main missions as fairly linear. There is no difficulty setting, only assists in the accessibility options. On pacing, a pattern emerges: like Red Dead Redemption 2, GTA 6 takes its time early on. If what you loved about GTA 5 was its fast, unhinged energy, expect an adjustment.

## Story and satire

Reviewers do not fully agree on the story. GamePro calls it the best in the series. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier, per Benzinga, puts it behind Red Dead Redemption 2 but ahead of GTA 5. TheGamer finds the satire slightly less biting than in earlier games in places.

## Tech and bugs

- **30 FPS:** GamePro says the frame rate is clearly noticeable on PS5 at first, with minor stutters in city traffic and during explosions.
- **Dips:** VGC sees them in the densest areas, but only rarely. Digital Foundry measured as low as 23 FPS on PS5 in a stress test, and Series S fluctuates the most.
- **Bugs:** GamePro mentions occasional clipping and one side mission that only continued after reloading a checkpoint. None of the reviews we read report anything serious.

## Our take

The list reads like a typical Rockstar game: weighty movement, linear missions, a lot of realism. These are not new problems but design choices that already split opinion on Red Dead Redemption 2. If they bothered you back then, they will bother you here too, just a little less.

Two points we would take more seriously. First, the 30 FPS if you are coming from fast shooters. Give it a few hours before you judge: GamePro explicitly says the frame rate stands out mostly at the beginning. Second, Series S, which struggles more than any other console. Everything else is the kind of rough edge that is almost impossible to avoid in a game this size.
