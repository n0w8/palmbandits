The GTA 6 release is right around the corner: on Thursday, November 19, 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Here is everything you need to know before launch, from the release time and preload to how much storage space you need.

**As of November 17, 2026**

## The key facts at a glance

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| Release date | Thursday, November 19, 2026 |
| Platforms | PS5 (including PS5 Pro), Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S |
| Release time | Midnight local time, overnight from Wednesday to Thursday (according to store listings) |
| Preload | Live since November 10 |
| Storage space | Up to 212 GB according to Rockstar |
| Prices (Europe) | Standard €79.99, Ultimate €99.99 |
| PC version | Not announced |
| Online mode | Not included at launch, the store listing describes GTA 6 as a single-player experience |

## What time does GTA 6 release?

According to the listings on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store, the game unlocks at **midnight local time**. In other words: 12:00 a.m. in your time zone, on the night of November 18 to November 19.

- Rockstar has not officially confirmed the exact release time, so expect small deviations.
- As always, New Zealand gets it first, with Europe following a few hours later.
- The preload won't let you play early: the data stays locked until release.

## Preload: download now, play Thursday

Rockstar moved the preload forward. Instead of the originally announced November 12, the pre-download started on **November 10**:

- **PS5:** from midnight local time
- **Xbox Series X|S:** from 00:00 GMT (1:00 a.m. in Central Europe)
- **Boxed version:** The box doesn't contain a disc, just a download code. According to Rockstar, it has been in stores since November 12. Redeem the code and you can preload as well.

Quick tip: PlayStation codes only work in the matching store region. If you're buying abroad, check this first.

According to reports, Rockstar already pushed two small updates before launch, on November 13 and November 16. There are no patch notes for them. Leave your console in rest mode with an internet connection so it stays up to date.

## 212 GB: how much space you need

Rockstar lists up to **212 GB of free storage**, depending on the console. A day-one patch may come on top of that, so plan for some headroom.

- **Xbox Series S with 512 GB:** Things get tight here. Clear out plenty of space beforehand.
- **PS5:** PS5 games only run from the internal SSD or an M.2 expansion drive. A USB drive is only good for storing other games.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Games run from the internal SSD or an official expansion card, USB drives are for storage only.

## Editions and prices

| Edition | Price (Europe) | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | €79.99 | Base game |
| Ultimate | €99.99 | Base game plus extra vehicles, weapons and clothing |

The Ultimate Edition is available digitally on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. According to Rockstar, you can upgrade from Standard to Ultimate at any time.

## Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

All pre-orders and purchases made **before November 20, 2026** include the Vintage Vice City Pack. For boxed copies, this only applies while supplies last. According to Rockstar, the pack includes:

- a two-tone 1955 Vapid Stanier plus a garage near Ocean Beach
- retro outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia
- a weapon livery inspired by Tommy Vercetti's palm tree shirt

Digital purchases on the PlayStation Store also come with one month of GTA+, according to the store listing. Heads up: the subscription renews automatically afterward unless you cancel.

## What about the PC version?

A PC version has **not been announced**. There is no release date and no pre-order. For context: GTA 5 came to PC around 19 months after its console launch, Red Dead Redemption 2 around 13 months. Anything you're reading about a PC release date right now is speculation.

## 30 FPS on all consoles

GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on all consoles, including the PS5 Pro. The Pro gets visual enhancements. Rockstar has not announced a 60 FPS mode so far.

## What you should do now

1. **Check the preload:** Look in your game library to make sure the download has actually finished.
2. **Free up space:** 212 GB plus headroom for the day-one patch.
3. **Turn on rest mode with internet:** That way updates install automatically.
4. **Redeem your box code:** If you already have the boxed version and haven't entered the code yet.
5. **Avoid spoilers:** Mute terms like "GTA 6 ending" on social media.
