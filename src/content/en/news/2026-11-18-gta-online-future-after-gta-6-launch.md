GTA 6 kicks off the series' next big chapter tomorrow, but without an online mode of its own. If you've spent years making money, running heists and collecting cars in Los Santos, one question is on your mind: is this the end of GTA Online? The short answer: no. Here's what's officially confirmed and what's still completely open.

**As of November 18, 2026**

## What's officially confirmed

Take-Two has spoken clearly twice in recent weeks:

- **Shareholder meeting on September 17:** According to TechRadar, Take-Two said it will continue to support GTA Online after the release of GTA 6 "and beyond."
- **Quarterly results on November 9:** In his prepared remarks, CEO Strauss Zelnick said, according to the transcript, that Rockstar will continue to support GTA Online with new content and ongoing events for its vast community.

There are also numbers that explain why Take-Two is sticking with it. According to Zelnick, the series grew by a double-digit percentage in both recurrent consumer spending and average monthly GTA+ members.

For context: GTA 6 launches as a standalone single-player game. Rockstar has not announced an online mode for it.

## What's still open

| Question | Status |
|---|---|
| Will GTA Online keep getting support? | Yes, according to Take-Two |
| For how long? | Not stated |
| Is an online mode coming for GTA 6? | Not announced |
| Can you transfer your character, money or vehicles? | Nothing announced |
| Price or business model of a new online mode? | Unknown |

Rockstar is keeping completely quiet here. When the video game show Kinda Funny asked about the next version of GTA Online during a preview at Rockstar's Edinburgh studio, Rockstar wouldn't say anything, according to Push Square. Bloomberg reporter Jason Schreier said, according to TechRadar, that Take-Two hasn't talked at all about what the new GTA Online will look like and isn't planning to anytime soon.

**Speculation:** Forums are already debating whether a GTA 6 online mode will arrive as a standalone paid product. There's no official basis for that, it's fan guesswork.

## The latest big update: The Kortz Center Heist

The summer update shows how seriously Rockstar takes the ongoing support. **The Kortz Center Heist** arrived on **July 14, 2026**, the first big heist update for GTA Online in six years according to GamesRadar. Per Shacknews, it's all about art and forgery:

- You add an Art Studio to your Mansion.
- A gifted counterfeiter helps you create copies of the paintings you plan to steal.
- The big score at the Kortz Center can be played solo or with up to three other players.
- Players without a Mansion got limited-time discounts and events.

## What this means for you

- **Your character stays:** GTA Online continues, and your progress isn't lost when GTA 6 releases. Take-Two hasn't named an end date.
- **No transfer promised:** Whether you'll ever be able to take anything into a new online game is open. Don't count on it.
- **Weekly events continue:** For an example of what they look like, see our story on the [event with 4x Freemode Challenges](/en/news/2026-11-15-gta-online-event-4x-freemode-challenges/).
- **Keep an eye on GTA+:** According to Vice, buying GTA 6 before November 20 gets you one month of GTA+ as part of the Vintage Vice City Pack. Check before it runs out whether you want to keep paying.

## Our take

Asked what players do once they're "done" with GTA Online, Zelnick essentially said he has literally never had to answer that question, because you can always keep going. It sounds like marketing, but it describes pretty well why GTA Online has been running for so many years.

To us, the situation is fairly clear: Rockstar will be running two games side by side over the coming months. GTA 6 gets the big stage, GTA Online gets ongoing operations with events. If you're waiting for a GTA 6 online mode, you'll need patience. Rockstar hasn't announced anything for launch, and we don't expect that to change during release week. That's our assessment, not information.

Our tip: don't let the launch hype stress you out. If you love GTA Online, you can keep playing there, and if you want to lose yourself in Leonida, you won't miss anything irreplaceable in Los Santos.
