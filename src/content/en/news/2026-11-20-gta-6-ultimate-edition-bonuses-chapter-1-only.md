If you bought the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition, don't expect all the extras right away, and a few of them only stick around at the start. According to Eurogamer, which cites IGN and the details shown in the game, three bonuses can only be used in Chapter 1. So the limit isn't a number of days. It depends on how far you are in the story.

## Usable in Chapter 1 only

- Jason's Dinka Enduro motorcycle
- the Crest Kayak
- the Ganado Retro Build for Jason's pickup, applied at a mod shop such as Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento, according to GamesRadar

The guides from Vice, Push Square and GamesRadar all say the same. None of the sources say whether you can get these back later.

## When the rest unlocks

According to Vice, Push Square and GamesRadar:

- **Chapter 1:** the shops Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie's, Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo and Sara's Unisex Salon (signature looks from Chapter 2), the Goodtime Gear collection, the PTT Youngin$ robberies, Jason's pistol
- **Chapter 2:** Lucia's pistol, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, the '67 Dominator Buggy and garage, the Classic Car Collection
- **Chapter 4:** Vice City Style, the Shitzu Squalo
- **Chapter 5:** the '95 Grotti Cheetah

Eurogamer and Vice estimate that reaching Chapter 2 takes upwards of 25 hours, and getting the Cheetah closer to 60.

## What's official

Rockstar's PlayStation Store description only says that new items are revealed with each chapter. It says nothing about some bonuses being usable for a limited time.

The real deadlines apply to the pre-order bonuses. Take-Two said every purchase before November 20 would get the Vintage Vice City Pack, and that window has now closed. As of Friday morning, the PlayStation Store lists the pack as a standalone add-on for $4.99 in the US and €4.99 in Germany. If you bought digitally by November 19 on PlayStation, you have until March 31, 2027 to claim your free month of GTA+. On Xbox, Vice says you get 180 days from your pre-order.

## What this means for you

- Get some use out of the Enduro, the kayak and the retro Ganado before you finish Chapter 1.
- Vice says Ultimate content is marked with a purple icon on the map, and Push Square says shop items carry a blue tag.
- Still weighing the upgrade? Check when the extra you want actually unlocks first.
- According to PlayStation, GTA+ renews at the regular price once the free month ends. Cancel in time if you don't want to keep paying.
