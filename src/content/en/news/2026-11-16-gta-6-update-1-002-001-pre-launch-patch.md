GTA 6 has received a second update before it even launches. Version 1.002.001 has been rolling out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since Monday, November 16, and weighs in at roughly 600 MB. Three days earlier, update 1.002.000 arrived at around 1 GB. Rockstar hasn't published patch notes for either of them.

**As of November 16, 2026**

## Both updates at a glance

| Version | Released | Size | Platforms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.002.000 | November 13 | about 1 GB (PS5 around 1.07 GB) | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |
| 1.002.001 | November 16 | about 600 MB | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |

For Xbox, a size of roughly 2.2 GB is also circulating for the first update. That figure only comes from individual reports and hasn't been confirmed.

## Rolled out in waves

What stands out is how Rockstar is distributing these updates. According to ComicBook and Beebom, only about half of the players with a finished preload got the first patch on PS5 at first. The rest followed roughly eleven hours later. The second update went the same way: when the first reports came in, only some PS5 and Xbox users had the new version.

The important part for you: update 1.002.000 is flagged as mandatory. Without it, the game won't start on November 19. If your console sits in rest mode with an internet connection, it will install automatically.

## What's inside?

Right now, nobody outside Rockstar knows. There are no patch notes, and Rockstar didn't explain the first update either. The writer at ComicBook guesses the second update might contain security fixes, but that's explicitly speculation.

Our take: small updates shortly before launch are normal for big games. A preload contains a build that had to be locked well before release day. Anything fixed after that ships as a patch. Rockstar staying quiet fits the studio's style; it rarely shares details in the run-up to a launch.

## Is there a day-one patch coming?

Nothing has been officially announced. Some outlets, including Netzwelt, already call update 1.002.000 a day-one patch because it has to be installed before the first launch. Rockstar itself doesn't use that term. Whether another update drops on November 19 is still open.

What it means for you: leave some headroom on your storage. Rockstar lists up to 212 GB for the game, and every update adds a little on top.

## How to check you're up to date

- **PS5:** Highlight GTA 6 in your game library, press the Options button and choose "Check for Update". Under "Information" you can see the installed version.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** In "My games & apps", highlight GTA 6, open "Manage game and add-ons" and check the "Updates" section.
- **Rest mode:** Leave your console in rest mode with an internet connection the night before release so it can grab any further updates by itself.

## What's still missing

Whether Rockstar will publish patch notes after launch is unclear. For bigger updates to its earlier games, the studio usually posted notes on its support site. That hasn't happened for the pre-launch updates so far.
