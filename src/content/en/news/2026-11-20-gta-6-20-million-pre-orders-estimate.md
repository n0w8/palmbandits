A new record figure has been doing the rounds since Thursday: GTA 6 was reportedly pre-ordered around 20 million times before release, according to IGN and Eurogamer. That number does not come from Rockstar or Take-Two, though. It is an analyst projection built on data from market research firm Sensor Tower.

**As of: November 20, 2026, early morning (CET).**

## Where the number comes from

- **Starting point:** By November 16, Sensor Tower had counted roughly 17.07 million copies.
- **Pace:** At that stage, IGN says, more than a million pre-orders were coming in per day on average. Extend that to launch day and you land at about 20 million.
- **Revenue:** Sensor Tower puts it above $1.6 billion, with about 75 percent on PS5.
- **Digital only:** Gadget Review notes that only digital pre-sales are counted. Per Game Informer there is no disc anyway, and store boxes contain a download code. Whether Sensor Tower tracks those codes is unclear.

## What is official

The record is confirmed, a unit count is not. On November 10, Sony and Microsoft both said no game had ever been pre-ordered more often in their stores. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told IGN that pre-orders were running well ahead of the company's original expectations. No number has followed, and per Reuters Zelnick refused to declare success early on the earnings call.

## Why the estimates jump around

Every figure comes from Sensor Tower, yet they differ depending on the date:

| As of | Copies | Revenue | Reported by |
|---|---|---|---|
| November 16 | 17.07 million | over $1.6 billion | IGN, Eurogamer |
| November 17 | over 15.8 million | $1.54 billion | Al Jazeera |
| November 19, evening | 24.58 million | about $2.36 billion | Reuters |

A later snapshot coming in lower than an earlier one shows how shaky these numbers are. The latest figure already includes launch day, so it is not a pure pre-order count. Speaking to Deadline, Sensor Tower put pre-order revenue through launch day at more than $2 billion. Gadget Review points out that the firm's method has not been independently verified.

## What this means for you

- **Read headlines carefully:** "20 million sold" is an estimate. Only Take-Two's own figures will settle it, and there is no official date for those yet.
- **Nothing changes for your game:** If something breaks online, check the PSN or Xbox status page before you reinstall anything.
