The current GTA Online event has been running since Thursday, November 12, and it's a strong one: 4x GTA$ and RP on Freemode Challenges, 3x on Stunt Races and double rewards on Contact Missions. According to Rockstar's announcement, the bonuses run until December 2, well past the GTA 6 launch.

**As of November 17, 2026.** As usual, the Podium vehicle, Prize Ride and discounts rotate on Thursday, November 19.

## Bonuses at a glance

| Bonus | Activity | Runs until |
|---|---|---|
| 4x GTA$ and RP | Freemode Challenges | December 2 |
| 3x GTA$ and RP | Stunt Race Series and Community Series | December 2 |
| 2x GTA$ and RP (4x with GTA+) | Contact Missions | December 2 |
| 2x GTA$ and RP | Sumo (Remix) | December 2 |

Contact Missions include Madrazo Dispatch Services, Gerald's Last Play, Simeon's Premium Deluxe Repo Work and Lamar's Lowrider missions, among others.

**Weekly Challenge:** Win two Stunt Races and collect GTA$100,000.

## Podium, Prize Ride and discounts

- **Podium vehicle:** Karin Everon. You get one free spin of the Lucky Wheel at the Diamond Casino per day, but winning comes down to luck.
- **Prize Ride:** Western Powersurge. To get it, you need to place in the top 4 of the LS Car Meet Series two days in a row.

**30% off these ten vehicles:**

- Obey I-Wagen
- Canis Castigator
- Karin Vivanite
- Dewbauchee Vagner
- Declasse Tahoma Coupe
- Obey Tailgater S
- Declasse Impaler LX
- Übermacht Rebla GTS
- Declasse Hotring Sabre
- MTL Wastelander

**Gun Van:** The Baseball Bat and Knife are free, and the Tactical SMG is 40% off. GTA+ members also save 40% on the Combat Shotgun and 80% on Body Armor.

## Is it worth it? Our take

**Freemode Challenges (4x):** These are the short competitions that start automatically in public sessions, such as the longest wheelie or the most near misses. Normally they're barely worth mentioning, but at 4x they're a nice bit of side income while you're in a session anyway.

**Contact Missions (2x, 4x with GTA+):** Ideal for new or returning players. No property needed, short missions, solid rewards. With GTA+, this is one of the most relaxed ways to make money this week.

**Stunt Races (3x):** If you enjoy racing, you can rake in serious cash here, especially combined with the GTA$100,000 Weekly Challenge.

**Discounts:** Nothing essential. If you've had your eye on a specific car from the list for a while, go for it. Everyone else is better off saving their money for businesses that earn money themselves.

## GTA+ this month

- Claim the Grotti Itali RSX for free, until December 9 according to Rockstar
- GTA$500,000 deposited every month
- Stacked bonus on Contact Missions (4x in total)

## What comes next

The weekly rotation with the Podium, Prize Ride and discounts changes on November 19, exactly on GTA 6 launch day. The big bonuses on Freemode Challenges, Stunt Races and Contact Missions stay active until December 2, though. If in doubt, check the in-game timer.
