Alongside the game, GTA VI: The Album drops on November 19, a collection of 34 new songs. Rockstar confirms this on GTA 6's official music page. Since November 14, an allegedly complete tracklist has also been circulating online, with names like Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey and Playboi Carti. That list is not official.

**As of November 14, 2026.** We clearly separate official facts from the leak below.

## What's official

Rockstar announced the album in September. The confirmed basics:

- **Release:** November 19, the same day as the game
- **Size:** 34 original songs
- **Formats:** streaming, CD and vinyl, in partnership with Atlantic Records according to GTABase
- **Important:** The album is not the same as the in-game radio, and it's not the score either

Rockstar's music page currently lists ten singles. Confirmed contributors include:

| Song | Artist |
|---|---|
| That's It | Yung Lean with Future and Metro Boomin |
| Rhyno | Travis Scott |
| Sexy Magic | CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, PinkPantheress, Fred again.. and Etienne de Crécy |
| Flower in a Hurricane | Justice with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard |
| Last Thing You Need | Morgan Wallen |
| Macacoa 2000 | Rauw Alejandro |
| Bright Lights, Big City | Keith Richards |

Rockstar also names Fuerza Regida, Cardi B and Tove Lo as part of the singles. During launch week, YouTube Music will also feature the soundtrack prominently on its homepage.

## The leaked tracklist (unconfirmed)

On November 14, several social media accounts shared a list of all 34 tracks. According to Dexerto, photos of the vinyl sleeves have surfaced as well. Notably, the positions of the singles already released match the order in the leak. That's not proof.

Besides the confirmed names, the list includes:

- Dua Lipa
- Lana Del Rey
- Playboi Carti
- Jennie
- Camila Cabello
- Zara Larsson
- Burna Boy
- Gunna and Young Thug

Some of these names don't come out of nowhere. In October, Rockstar already confirmed Lana Del Rey and Burna Boy as hosts of in-game radio stations, along with Morgan Wallen. Whether they also have songs on the album remains open until release.

## Justice's score arrives December 11

Separately from the album, Rockstar is releasing the game's score. Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score comes from the French duo Justice and drops on December 11. According to Rockstar, it features 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks on streaming, CD and vinyl. Two pieces are already out: "Flower in a Hurricane" and "Pusher" featuring Gesaffelstein.

## And the in-game radio?

The radio is its own thing. In October, Rockstar revealed six stations, including Flash FM for pop and dance, The Chamber 106.6 for metal and Cocoteo FM for Latin music. Rockstar hasn't published a complete list of all stations and songs yet.

## Our take

Rockstar is running a soundtrack strategy you'd usually expect from a blockbuster movie: a star-studded album, a separate score and a radio lineup inside the game. The first singles have been on streaming services since September, keeping the game in the conversation even with people who don't play. How powerful GTA's music can be shows in an example from the first trailer: according to CNBC, Spotify streams of Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road" jumped by more than 37,000 percent afterwards.

Whether the leak is accurate, we'll know by November 19 at the latest. Until then: the names above are plausible but unconfirmed.
