The wait was worth it. GTA 6 reviews went live at 11:00 GMT, and the result is historic: on Metacritic, Grand Theft Auto VI for PS5 sits at a **Metascore of 99**. That is the highest score the site has ever shown, tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

**As of: November 18, 2026, afternoon.** More reviews are still coming in, so the average can still shift slightly.

## The numbers at a glance

- **Metascore:** 99 on PS5. At 11:16 GMT it was based on 109 critic reviews, all positive, not one mixed or negative.
- **Record:** Tied with Zelda: Ocarina of Time (99), until now the only game with that score.
- **Series record:** GTA IV led with 98, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 scored 97.
- **Embargo:** Reviews went live at midnight New Zealand time, which is 11:00 GMT, as Insider Gaming had reported in advance.

## Who gave what

Perfect scores dominate. About 90 of the first 109 reviews on Metacritic are 100s, including:

- **100:** IGN, GameSpot, Game Informer, Edge, Eurogamer, The Washington Post, IGN Germany, PC Games
- **98:** GamePro (Germany)
- **96:** GIGA
- **95:** 4Players
- **90 (lowest score):** among others The New York Times and IGN Japan

## What the reviews praise

- **The world:** Leonida, with Vice City and the other regions, is seen as the centrepiece. Every area feels distinct, and there are far more enterable buildings.
- **Weather and tech:** The new dynamic weather system is praised, and the picture mostly runs smoothly.
- **Missions and characters:** Jason and Lucia carry the story, missions are called varied and the writing strong, with unexpected twists.
- **Controls:** The controls are described as precise and responsive.

## What the reviews criticise

- **Story pacing:** Some reviews say the story loses momentum at times because there is so much content. PC Games also says the plot is not the game's high point.
- **Crowded Vice City:** Heavy traffic and crowds slow down some missions.
- **30 FPS:** The game runs at 30 frames per second on every console. Digital Foundry measured occasional dips below that, though the picture stays smooth overall.

## Our take

A 99 average across more than a hundred reviews is not just "good", it is a consensus the industry almost never sees. The criticisms are the expected ones: a game this big has quiet stretches, and 30 FPS will bother anyone coming from fast shooters. For tonight that means: if you pre-ordered, you can relax. And if you were still on the fence, these reviews leave you very few reasons to stay there.

The next thing to watch is the user score. According to Notebookcheck it only opens 36 hours after release, so there are no early ratings from people who could not have played the game yet.
