The wait is over. GTA 6 has been out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since Thursday, November 19. Rockstar confirmed the worldwide release on its Newswire, and Xbox followed with an Xbox Wire post at 14:00 UTC. Here is how day one went.

**As of: November 20, 2026, early morning (CET).**

## What is official

- **Release:** According to Rockstar, Grand Theft Auto VI is now playable worldwide on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Unlock:** Midnight local time in each region. AP reports that New Zealand was among the very first countries to get it.
- **Rockstar's thanks:** Co-founder Sam Houser, who almost never speaks publicly, shared a launch statement through GamesRadar+. He says the studio is grateful, hopes above all that you have fun, and looks forward to "many more years" with players. He mentions no plans for a new online mode.

## How launch day went

- **PSN outages:** TechRepublic counts two PlayStation Network disruptions, on November 18 and 19. Mein-MMO saw lots of connection complaints on Wednesday evening European time, but the midnight unlock in Germany went largely smoothly. On Thursday, outage monitor IsDown logged about 24 minutes of degraded service from 05:34 UTC. Sony has not said whether GTA traffic was the cause.
- **New Zealand trick:** Around 2 million Xbox players in the US and UK switched their console region to New Zealand and started on November 18. The estimate comes from Ampere Analysis, as reported by Eurogamer and others.
- **Streaming:** Citing Streamlabs and Streamscharts data, IGN reports that GTA 6 became the most popular game in Twitch and Kick history within about a day. Peak viewership across all tracked platforms passed 7.25 million.
- **Sales:** No official numbers yet. According to Reuters, Sensor Tower estimates about 24.6 million copies sold and roughly $2.36 billion in revenue by Thursday evening.

## Still unanswered

When Take-Two will publish its own sales data is unknown. There is still no official word on a PC version or a new online mode.

## What this means for you

- **Game won't start?** Check the PSN status page first. If everything is green, TechRepublic says the cause is more likely a download, licence or settings issue. Sony suggests restarting the download and restoring licences in your account settings.
- **Before the next outage:** Turn on "Console Sharing and Offline Play" on your primary PS5. According to Mein-MMO, the game can then start even while PSN is down.
