YouTube is turning the GTA 6 launch into a live event: the platform will run a 24-hour livestream with well-known gaming creators and describes it as a collaboration with Rockstar Games. YouTube announced the plan on November 12. It kicks off with a pre-show at 11 PM Eastern on November 18, and the main stream starts an hour later.

**As of November 12, 2026**

## The schedule

| Segment | US Eastern | UK (GMT) | Central Europe (CET) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-show with RDCWorld | Nov 18, 11 PM | Nov 19, 4 AM | Nov 19, 5 AM |
| 24-hour stream begins | Nov 19, 12 AM | Nov 19, 5 AM | Nov 19, 6 AM |
| Finale with live coverage from Miami | Nov 20, 12 AM | Nov 20, 5 AM | Nov 20, 6 AM |

The stream runs on the official youtube.com/youtube channel. The YouTube homepage gets a custom GTA VI-themed logo that takes you straight to it. The production partner is the agency Entropico.

## The creators taking part

In its own blog post, YouTube names Caylus, Zhong, Kat, Jelly, Rivers, Jake Baldino and Cinna, among others. According to Insider Gaming, 31 streamers and creators have been announced in total, including Kinda Funny Games, Windah Basudara and Total Gaming. That makes the stream properly international, with creators from the US, Indonesia and India among others.

## Extras: wanted stars and a Vice City filter

YouTube is launching several features around the stream:

- **Live gifts:** five limited-edition, game-themed gifts, such as wanted stars that pop up on screen during streams. On selected creator streams, eligible viewers can send a GTA VI gift for free while supplies last.
- **Shorts effect:** "GTAVI: Visit Vice City" lets you place yourself in front of iconic backdrops from the game.
- **YouTube Music:** During launch week, the official soundtrack and a playlist with music from the whole series are featured on the homepage.

## The numbers behind the hype

YouTube also shared its own data with the announcement. Videos about the GTA series have racked up more than 900 billion views in total. Videos about GTA 6 alone passed 40 billion views before the game is even out.

How big the appetite for live content is already showed during the major gameplay presentation in August. According to analytics firm Streams Charts, reaction streams peaked at almost 3.97 million concurrent viewers across Twitch, YouTube and Kick.

## Kai Cenat already warmed up

One of the biggest streamers has already done his homework. On November 5, Kai Cenat started a marathon through all five major predecessors in release order: GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and GTA V. According to Beebom, it took him just over 130 hours. After launch, he plans to play GTA 6 to 100 percent, also live. He asked his community not to post story spoilers and warned anyone who wants to experience the story themselves to steer clear of his stream.

## Our take

A platform running a 24-hour livestream for a single game is unusual and shows how much GTA 6 matters to the entire industry. For you as a player, though, it's a double-edged offer: if you plan to play yourself, it's better to avoid the stream on launch day. With 24 hours of gameplay, the risk of seeing story moments you'd rather discover yourself is high.

Our tip: tune in once you've finished the story, or if you just want to soak up the atmosphere. If you want to stay spoiler-free, mute terms like "GTA 6 ending" on social media.
