Tonight is the night: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026. Because the launch follows local time, some regions get to play much earlier than others. Here is the full schedule, with every unlock converted to UTC so you can work out your own time.

**As of November 18, 2026**

## The key points

- The game unlocks at **midnight local time** on November 19, region by region.
- **New Zealand** goes first: 11:00 UTC on Wednesday, November 18.
- **United States:** midnight Eastern for the whole country, which is 05:00 UTC on November 19.
- There are roughly **19 hours** between the first and the last region.
- The times come from PlayStation Store timestamps analyzed by the fan site GTAVice. Several outlets reported them consistently in September.

On its own game page, Rockstar has only confirmed the date, November 19, 2026. The times are store data, so small deviations on the night are possible.

## A special case: the United States

According to the store data, **midnight Eastern applies to the entire country**. If you live in California, you can start at 9:00 p.m. local time on Wednesday. Mexico and Central America are last in line, at 06:00 UTC.

## The 19-hour spoiler gap

The staggered launch means one thing above all: players in New Zealand and Australia are in the game from Wednesday afternoon UTC, many hours before Europe and the Americas. Expect the first clips and screenshots to surface well before your own unlock.

Our advice if you want to experience the story untouched:

- **Mute keywords:** "GTA 6 ending", "GTA 6 story", character names.
- **Avoid streams:** Don't click thumbnails, ignore your recommendations.
- **Brief your group chats:** Anyone who plays earlier keeps it to themselves.

## PS5: your account decides, not the clock

On the PlayStation Store, what counts is the **region of your account**, not where your console is.

- Changing your console's clock does nothing.
- A copy bought on a US account unlocks on US time, wherever the console is.
- Playing earlier would require an account from an earlier region. We advise against it: your purchase is tied to your existing account.

## Xbox: the New Zealand trick is unconfirmed

Some sites report that Xbox players can play earlier by switching their console region to New Zealand. We found no official confirmation from Microsoft or Rockstar, and the timestamp list comes from the PlayStation Store. We therefore treat the trick as **unconfirmed**. Plan for midnight local time on Xbox and you won't be disappointed.

## Preload: helps with downloading, not with playing early

The preload has been live since November 10. It makes sure you don't spend hours downloading at midnight. It won't let you play earlier, though: the data stays locked until the unlock. That applies equally to the Standard Edition, the Ultimate Edition and the boxed version with a download code.

If you haven't checked your preload yet, now is the time. Everything on storage space, patches and the box code is in our [overview of release time, preload and 212 GB](/en/news/2026-11-17-gta-6-release-time-preload-everything-you-need/).

## Our take

Nothing changes about the rule: midnight local time on November 19. What stands out is how early the rest of the world starts. A head start of half a day or more for New Zealand is an eternity for a game this size, especially for anyone who wants to stay spoiler-free. Keep your evening free, silence notifications and leave your console in rest mode with internet, and you'll be in the best position at midnight.
