GTA 6 launches tomorrow, but only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. For everyone who plays on PC, the big question remains: when is it our turn? The honest answer is still that nobody knows officially. Here's what Take-Two has actually said, what has only been reported and how we read it.

**As of November 18, 2026**

## Where things stand

| Question | Status |
|---|---|
| PC version announced? | No |
| Official PC release date? | No |
| Platforms at launch | PS5, Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S |
| Reported PC window | First quarter of 2027, according to GamesBeat (unconfirmed) |
| Official PC system requirements | None |

## What Take-Two said on November 9

Ten days before launch, Take-Two presented its quarterly results. Platforms came up during the call with analysts. According to the transcript, CEO Strauss Zelnick said GTA VI is available **on two consoles** when it launches on November 19.

Asked about new versions and platforms, he said that kind of information **will always come from the labels**, in this case Rockstar itself. Elsewhere in the call, he noted in general terms that experiences on console and PC tend to be roughly the same these days. He did not tie that remark to GTA 6.

In other words: Take-Two isn't announcing anything, but it isn't denying anything either. The ball is in Rockstar's court.

## Zelnick's line: console first

The reasoning has been the same for months. In a Bloomberg interview in May, reported by GameStar, Zelnick said Rockstar always launches on consoles first and has always done so. On the PC question, he only said they would see how things go.

What's interesting is how he describes the importance of PC himself. According to GameStar, PC made up about 5 percent of NBA 2K sales in 2007. For big titles today, he said, it's 45 to 50 percent. Back in 2024, Zelnick had already said that Rockstar has its own approach to platforms and "will make more announcements in due time."

## The early 2027 report (rumor)

**Heads up, unconfirmed:** In mid-October, the trade outlet GamesBeat reported that the PC version is planned for the first quarter of 2027, so by the end of March at the latest. According to VGC, the report cites four anonymous sources said to be familiar with Rockstar's plans.

Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed that window. The November 9 call didn't bring a date either. We're treating the report as a rumor, even though GamesBeat isn't some anonymous social media account.

## For comparison: how long Rockstar took before

| Game | Console release | PC release | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| GTA 5 | September 17, 2013 | April 14, 2015 | about 19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | October 26, 2018 | November 5, 2019 | just over a year |

If the report is accurate, GTA 6 would reach PC much faster than GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2: after a few months instead of a year or more.

## Our take

Three things are clear to us:

1. **There is no official PC version.** Any offers, keys or pre-orders for GTA 6 on PC you're seeing right now are not official. Stay away.
2. **There are no official system requirements.** Lists of graphics cards and processors circulating online are guesses. Don't buy new hardware for them now.
3. **A PC release is likely, but not announced.** Zelnick's own figures on the PC share show how important the platform has become. When Rockstar serves it is Rockstar's call, and the announcement will come from there.

We think it's plausible that Rockstar won't talk about PC until after the console launch. That's our assessment, not information.
