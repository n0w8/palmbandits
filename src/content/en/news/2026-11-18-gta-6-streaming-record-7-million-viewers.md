GTA 6 is only playable in New Zealand, Australia, Japan and South Korea so far, and it has already rewritten streaming history. According to preliminary Streams Charts data, livestreams of the game peaked at **7.18 million concurrent viewers**. That takes the launch record away from Valorant, and not by a small margin.

**As of: November 18, 2026, 4:53 pm CET (15:53 GMT).** These figures are preliminary. Europe and the Americas only join tonight.

## The numbers by platform

| Platform | Peak concurrent viewers |
|---|---|
| All platforms combined | 7,183,979 |
| Twitch | 3.06 million |
| YouTube | 2.74 million |
| Kick | 1.36 million |

Streams Charts says the latest jump came when the game unlocked in Japan and South Korea at 15:00 GMT. Just half an hour after the New Zealand unlock at 11:00 GMT, TheGamer was already counting more than 2.5 million viewers in the Twitch category alone.

## The records that fell

- **Launch record across platforms:** Valorant held it with 1,737,267 viewers during its closed beta in April 2020. GTA 6 is now about 5.4 million ahead.
- **Single-player record on Twitch:** Hogwarts Legacy peaked at around 1.28 million in 2023, according to Guinness World Records. GTA 6 passed that within roughly 30 minutes.
- **Twitch categories:** At 3.06 million, GTA VI is now the second-biggest gaming category Twitch has ever seen by peak viewers. Only League of Legends is ahead, with 3,111,023 viewers in November 2022. Fortnite (3.04 million) has been overtaken.

For context, here is how other big launches on the Streams Charts list compare: Cyberpunk 2077 reached 1.32 million, Elden Ring 1.04 million. GTA 6 is at five to seven times those peaks.

## The biggest streams

The largest single stream, according to Streams Charts, belonged to the channel HoneyMad with 576,313 viewers on Twitch. Brazilian streamer alanzoka hit 313,548, a personal best. Shortly after the New Zealand launch, TheGamer had alanzoka, xQc and TheBurntPeanut at the top.

## Bigger than the analysts expected

The forecasts show just how big this is. Oppenheimer analyst Martin Yang had expected a Twitch peak of around 2 million, according to Investing.com. It went past 3 million before a single major Western market had unlocked. For the big gameplay showcase in August, Streams Charts counted just under 3.97 million viewers across all platforms.

## Our take

These numbers are more than trivia. They show that GTA 6 is already the biggest live event the games industry has ever had. League of Legends still holds the all-time Twitch record for now, and we expect that to change tonight once Europe unlocks at midnight local time and the US at midnight Eastern. That is not guaranteed, though, because the audience will be spread across more time zones and platforms.

For you, the hype has one obvious downside: with millions watching, story moments end up in clips and thumbnails fast. If you want to stay spoiler-free, keep away from Twitch, Kick and the YouTube home page tonight.
