Since the review embargo lifted today, GTA 6's length is no longer a matter of leaks. Critics needed roughly 50 to 65 hours for the main story, and anyone who spends time on side activities quickly passes 100 hours. That makes GTA 6 clearly longer than GTA 5, and at least on par with Red Dead Redemption 2 for the story alone.

**As of: November 18, 2026, 4:52 pm CET (15:52 GMT).**

## Playtimes from the reviews

| Review | Main story | Total time played |
|---|---|---|
| GamePro (Germany) | about 50 hours | around 100 hours (Tobias Veltin), over 120 hours (Valentin Aschenbrenner) |
| VGC | around 60 hours without side content | not specified |
| PC Games (Germany) | about 65 hours | close to 100 hours with thorough exploring |
| TheGamer | not specified | nearly 100 hours |
| IGN | not specified | over 120 hours across three weeks |
| Bloomberg (Jason Schreier) | not specified | over 45 hours across three weeks |

The IGN figure comes from TheGamer's round-up, the Jason Schreier figure from a Benzinga report. GamePro's Tobias Veltin split his hundred or so hours between the base PS5 (close to 70) and PS5 Pro (30).

## What the numbers mean

The range looks wide, but it is easy to explain. Stick to the main missions and you finish in 50 to 65 hours. Add side missions, minigames, races and just roaming the world, and you are closer to double that. VGC says, in effect, that the open world holds hundreds of hours of content.

Output Lag went through 18 reviews and came out with the same picture: the main story lands between 50 and 80 hours, with around 60 the most common figure. Completionists should expect 150 hours or more.

One more thing pushes the hours up: GTA 6 has multiple endings. According to VGC, how the story ends depends partly on a relationship meter between Jason and Lucia. PC Games says it will dig into the differences between the endings over the coming weeks.

## Compared with GTA 5 and Red Dead 2

Going by community averages on HowLongToBeat, GTA 5's story takes about 32 hours and Red Dead Redemption 2's about 50. At 50 to 65 hours, GTA 6 sits well above GTA 5 and at least level with RDR2.

Rockstar itself only ever gave one hint. Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, told The New York Times his playthrough took about 80 hours, including optional objectives that affect the story. That lines up remarkably well with the reviews. The leak from mid-November that claimed a 50-hour story also looks accurate based on GamePro's figure.

## What about the platinum or 100 percent?

There are no reliable numbers yet. The 200 hours for every trophy is still an estimate. Reviewers had about three weeks and focused on story and world, not collectibles. Real averages will only arrive once enough players have finished.

## Our take

For review conditions, these numbers are on the lean side. Critics play under deadline and with purpose. If you wander around like most of us will, expect the story to take longer than 50 hours. Budgeting 60 to 80 hours to the credits is safer, so plan your first few weekends accordingly.

It is not all wall-to-wall action, though. In Output Lag's sample, 8 of 18 reviews criticise the pacing, for example a slow opening or downtime between jobs.
