Barely a day after launch, players are chasing Bigfoot and UFOs across Leonida. In interviews with Kotaku and VideoGamesChronicle (VGC), Rob Nelson of Rockstar North explained why the studio plants mysteries like these but skips a theme park like Disney World.

## Why Bigfoot and UFOs?

Kotaku introduces Nelson as Rockstar North's head of production. His reasons:

- **A taste for the strange:** The team loves this stuff, and real Florida is odd enough on its own.
- **Toying with players:** You should never be sure whether something is truly supernatural or just a man in a Bigfoot mask.
- **Team size:** These days there are enough people to handle details like these.
- **A busy wilderness:** The countryside should offer as much to discover as the cities.

## What players have found so far

**Spoiler warning:** Rough locations only, nothing about the story.

- **"Bigfoot" is the Skunk Ape:** According to VGC and Eurogamer, it is Florida's swamp take on Bigfoot, found out in the swamps. VGC says it kicks off a longer questline, and the first sighting is not a main story mission.
- **UFO encounter:** Eurogamer describes a random night-time event in the woods of Kelly County that only unlocks after the first chapter. It earns you a bronze trophy.

Bigfoot has history here: in San Andreas he was only a fan rumour, GamesRadar notes, and Rockstar later picked the idea up in GTA 5 and GTA Online.

## Why there is no Disney World

Speaking to VGC, which introduces him as co-head of Rockstar North, Nelson gives these reasons:

- **Too much work:** The team had no capacity for a theme park.
- **No story reason:** There was no strong narrative reason to send Jason and Lucia there.
- **Where do you stop?** A Rockstar park would have to work properly, rides included. When would it be enough?
- **Limits:** Nelson gives a similar reason for leaving out darts and bowling. Not even Rockstar has unlimited time and money.

GameSpot adds that the map stays within Vice City and its surroundings and does not reach Orlando, home of the real Disney World. Still, as VGC points out, the original GTA Vice City advertised a park called Fun World.

## What this means for you

Keep your eyes open, especially away from the cities. For the UFO encounter, Eurogamer says you need to have finished the first chapter, and shooting at Bigfoot gets you nowhere. If anyone promises you a secret theme park in Leonida: according to Rockstar, there is no Disney World counterpart.
