GTA 6 has barely launched, and Cyberleek is back. On Thursday the hacker group announced that it had put a complete development build of the game for Xbox into circulation. VideoGamesChronicle (VGC), Eurogamer and PC Gamer reported the claim, which nobody has independently confirmed so far.

**As of: Friday morning, November 20, 2026.**

## What Cyberleek is claiming

- **The build:** The group describes it as an Xbox version dated November 2026. According to Eurogamer, the group's site lists it at 209 GB.
- **No executable:** The main executable is not included, so the files can't be played as they are. Cyberleek says it will publish that file once a countdown runs out, or sooner if the group's own crypto token hits a market cap of $15 million. PC Gamer reports the file is due on November 23 at the latest.
- **Big promises:** The group says emulation will have the game running within a week, with a PS5 build as its fallback.

## What is confirmed and what isn't

The only confirmed part is that Cyberleek is publicly making these claims. Eurogamer looked at the site but could not verify that the files actually exist. PC Gamer and VideoCardz have no confirmation the build is genuine either. On the emulation talk, Notebookcheck points out that no working Xbox Series X emulator exists right now.

The group is not new to this, though. For months it has been posting gameplay clips that VGC describes as authentic, while asking fans for crypto donations. According to VGC and Eurogamer, Take-Two has already sought user data from Discord to identify the leaker. As of Friday morning, we have found no response from Rockstar or Take-Two to this latest claim.

## What this means for you

However tempting it is, don't download any of it, for two reasons.

- **Malware:** Back in August, Malwarebytes found fake GTA 6 sites handing out a password stealer instead of a game. The first sample showed up one day after the first Cyberleek clips appeared. In our view, a supposedly late executable that lots of people are waiting for makes perfect bait for scammers.
- **Legal trouble:** Downloading leaked game files is illegal. With file sharing, you usually upload the data to others at the same time, which makes you a distributor too.

You aren't missing out: GTA 6 has been officially available on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since November 19. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version, so any "GTA 6 PC download" you see is not official.
