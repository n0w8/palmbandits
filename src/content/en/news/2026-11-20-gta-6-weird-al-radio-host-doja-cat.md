One day after launch, a lot of the GTA 6 chatter is about the radio. "Weird Al" Yankovic has confirmed he hosts one of the stations, while Doja Cat went on X to complain that her music is missing.

## Weird Al behind the mic at Vice City FM

Yankovic confirmed it himself: in a Bluesky post on November 19, he said he hosts Vice City FM, the game's "non-stop '80s" station. According to PC Gamer, his character goes by DJ Al and shares the show with actress Brigitte Nielsen. PC Gamer adds that the credits Rockstar put out at launch back both of them up.

- **More than a DJ:** By his own account, Yankovic also wrote some of the commercials and voices the Burger Shot ads.
- **None of his own songs:** GamesRadar reports the credits also list him for original music, yet none of his tracks play on any station. Kotaku came to the same conclusion while playing.
- **More famous voices:** According to PC Gamer, Jeremy Irons hosts Kaleidoscope FM.

Kotaku had Rockstar confirm the role. In our radio guide, Vice City FM was until now just a name from leaks. The station is now confirmed.

## Doja Cat: no songs, lots of frustration

On November 19, as Kotaku and GamesRadar report, Doja Cat posted on X that she wishes her music were in GTA 6 and is "so f***in bitter" about it. In two more posts she went at Rockstar directly in far cruder words, while also professing her love for the game and adding "you broke my heart".

- **No tracks of hers:** Kotaku says no Doja Cat song plays on the in-game radio.
- **A GTA connection:** Kotaku points out that she name-checks GTA 6 in her song "All Mine".
- **Rockstar:** Neither Kotaku nor GamesRadar mentions any reply from the studio.

## Serious or just for fun?

Hard to say. GamesRadar notes that Doja Cat is known for over-the-top joke posts. Kotaku thinks Rockstar could still add her music in an update. Nothing has been announced, and both points are the outlets' own takes.

## What this means for you

For the '80s banter, tune in to Vice City FM, and listen out for the Burger Shot ads, because that is Yankovic too. There are no Weird Al songs on the radio, though, and according to Kotaku no Doja Cat tracks play on any station right now. Whether an update changes that remains open.
