If you plan to play GTA 6 on PS5 tonight, the pre-load alone is not enough. Sony has pushed out a new console system update and says outright that it is required for GTA 6. Without it, the game will not start. Install it now rather than at five to midnight.

**As of: November 18, 2026, 4:51 pm CET (15:51 GMT).**

## What Sony said

The PlayStation Support US account posted the notice on X on November 17 at 8:25 pm ET. In short: Sony Interactive has released a PS5 system software update that is needed before you can play Grand Theft Auto VI, and players should download it before midnight on November 19 to be ready for day one.

The key points:

- **Mandatory:** GTA 6 will not boot on a PS5 without it.
- **Version:** According to VICE, you need **system software 14.23.00 or later**.
- **Separate download:** The system update has nothing to do with the game's own patch. You need both.
- **Contents:** Sony gives no details. Kotaku suspects extra protection against unlocking pre-loaded copies early, or against jailbroken consoles.

## Why the version number kept changing

This has been brewing for a while. Back in October, MP1st reported that GTA 6 would demand a minimum firmware version, which at the time was 14.10. The background is the PS5 jailbreak scene, which has cracked older firmware. Reports since then have put the required version higher more than once. The exact number does not really matter to you: just make sure your console is fully up to date.

## How to install it

1. On your PS5, open **Settings**, then **System**, then **System Software**.
2. Choose the system software update option and let the console check online.
3. After the restart, the same menu shows which version is installed.
4. While you are there, switch on automatic downloads and installs in rest mode, so the console grabs any further updates overnight.

If your connection is playing up, Sony also offers the system software as a download on playstation.com for installing from a USB drive. It takes longer, but works without the console reaching PSN.

## What about the game's day one patch?

The current version is 1.002.001, which started rolling out on November 16. On PS5 it is around 600 MB, and Kotaku describes it as the day one patch. Rockstar still has not published patch notes. As of this afternoon we have not found any report of a further patch today. To check, highlight the game in your library, press Options and pick "Check for Update".

So far there is no similar mandatory console update reported for Xbox.

## Is PSN holding up?

There was a partial outage on November 11 while the pre-load was running. According to PlayFront, account management and the PlayStation Store were down at times, while online multiplayer kept working. We have not found a confirmed major outage around the launches in New Zealand, Australia and Japan as of this afternoon. That could change tonight when Europe comes online at once.

## Our take

A mandatory update the evening before launch is annoying, but it makes sense. Rockstar and Sony clearly want to stop pre-loaded copies being unlocked early or played on hacked consoles. If you do it now, it takes a few minutes. At midnight, millions of consoles will be pulling data at the same time, and five minutes can easily turn into thirty.

Our plan for tonight: system update now, check the game update, then leave the console in rest mode with internet on. That way all you have to do at launch is pick up the controller.
