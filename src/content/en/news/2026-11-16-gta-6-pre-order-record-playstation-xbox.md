It's official: GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game in the history of the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. Sony and Microsoft confirmed the pre-order record almost simultaneously on November 10, nine days before the November 19 release.

**As of November 17, 2026**

## What Sony and Microsoft said

- **PlayStation:** The PlayStation Blog says Grand Theft Auto VI is now the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history.
- **Xbox:** Microsoft also calls it the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store. At the same time, Xbox urged all buyers to start their preload as early as possible because demand is so high.
- **Hard numbers:** Neither Sony nor Microsoft shared any.

## Take-Two: pre-orders far above expectations

Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, is in a great mood as well. On its quarterly earnings call, CEO Strauss Zelnick said pre-orders were far above the company's original expectations. According to CNBC, he also said that expectations had been high and that they were being clearly exceeded so far.

More key figures from the reports:

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| GTA 5 copies sold | Around 235 million | Take-Two |
| Net bookings forecast for the fiscal year | Over $8 billion | Take-Two, via Game Developer |
| GTA 6 sales forecast through March 2027 | Up to 40 million | Morgan Stanley, via CNBC |

## Be careful with pre-order numbers

Plenty of specific pre-order numbers are circulating online. Almost all of them come from estimates by market research firms such as Sensor Tower, and they vary wildly: depending on the report and cutoff date, they range from around 4 million to more than 12 million. According to reports, Take-Two only plans to share official figures at launch. Until then, the rule is simple: anything with an exact number is an estimate, not a fact.

## What this means for you

A pre-order record also means that a huge number of people will want to start playing at the same time on launch night.

- **Finish your preload now:** If you wait until Thursday to download, you could end up stuck in a download traffic jam.
- **Grab updates in advance:** Leave your console in rest mode with an internet connection.
- **Be patient with PSN and Xbox Live:** Launches this big can cause brief hiccups. Don't panic and reinstall.
