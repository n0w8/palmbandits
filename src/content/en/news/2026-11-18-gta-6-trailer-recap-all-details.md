For almost three years, trailers were the only real source for GTA 6. From tonight, only the game itself counts. Time for a look back: here are all of Rockstar's official videos, their songs, their records and, above all, what they actually confirmed.

**As of November 18, 2026**

## Every video at a glance

| Date | Video | What it showed |
|---|---|---|
| December 2023 | Trailer 1 | Leonida, Vice City, Lucia and Jason, a 2025 release window |
| May 6, 2025 | Trailer 2 | Focus on Jason and Lucia, captured entirely on PS5 |
| August 27, 2026 | Extended Look | About 26 minutes, the first in-depth gameplay |
| October 15, 2026 | Launch Trailer | The Keys, heists, a focus on the duo |

There were two delays in between: in early May 2025 from 2025 to May 26, 2026, and in November 2025 to November 19, 2026.

## Trailer 1: a record-breaking start

The first trailer was planned for December 5, 2023. After a leak, Rockstar put the video online a few hours early. It barely mattered:

- **90,421,491 views** in the first 24 hours on YouTube
- **Three Guinness World Records:** most-viewed video game reveal in 24 hours, most-liked video game trailer, and most-viewed non-music YouTube video in 24 hours
- **Song:** "Love Is a Long Road" by Tom Petty

In terms of content, Trailer 1 introduced Leonida, Vice City, Lucia and Jason, and still named 2025 as the release window for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Trailer 2: story, characters, 475 million

Trailer 2 followed on May 6, 2025, a few days after the first delay.

- **Captured on PS5:** According to Rockstar, entirely in-game on a PlayStation 5, in equal parts gameplay and cutscenes.
- **Reach:** Rockstar reported 475 million views across all platforms in 24 hours. That's Rockstar's own figure, published by The Hollywood Reporter, not an independent measurement.
- **Song:** "Hot Together" by The Pointer Sisters.
- **Story:** At the start, Lucia leaves prison together with Jason.

On the same day, Rockstar introduced the supporting characters and the regions of Leonida on its official website. Who's who is covered in our guides to the [characters](/en/guides/gta-6-jason-lucia-characters-guide/) and the [map](/en/guides/gta-6-map-leonida-vice-city-regions/).

## Extended Look: 26 minutes of gameplay

On August 27, 2026, Rockstar showed an Extended Look of about 26 minutes, first on Netflix and a few hours later on YouTube. Members of the press had already visited Rockstar North the month before and were allowed to publish their impressions afterward.

The video and the previews confirmed most of the gameplay systems we know today:

- **Six wanted stars** instead of five as in GTA 5
- **Criminal Profile:** The game keeps track of how you behave as a criminal
- **Character switching:** When Jason and Lucia are together, you switch instantly
- **Slow-motion aiming:** A Dead Eye-style mode highlights weak spots
- **Downtime:** Races, jet skis, weightlifting, scuba diving and more

During the previews, Rob Nelson, co-studio head of Rockstar North, told Kinda Funny: no monetization in the game and no generative AI. We've collected every system in detail in our [tips for your first hours](/en/guides/gta-6-first-hours-tips-gameplay-systems/).

## Launch Trailer: story over systems

The launch trailer arrived on Rockstar's YouTube channel on October 15, 2026. Instead of mechanics, it puts Jason and Lucia front and center:

- **The Keys:** The opening shot shows an area that earlier trailers barely touched.
- **Heists:** Several robberies, one apparently ending in a helicopter getaway gone wrong.
- **Police chase:** A pursuit cut in between the heist scenes.
- **The duo:** An intimate scene between Jason and Lucia.

**Interpretations, not confirmed:** Eurogamer spotted what may be an ankle monitor on Lucia's leg and reads a reverse-charge call Jason receives as a call from jail. That fits with previews in August reporting that Lucia's ankle monitor plays a central role in the campaign.

Rockstar itself describes the story as a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida.

## The sound: Justice instead of a licensed hit

The launch trailer is the first to use a new song rather than a well-known licensed track: **"Flower in a Hurricane"** by Justice, featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard. According to Rockstar, the French duo Justice is also composing the game's original score.

According to RouteNote, two albums are planned:

- **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album** with 34 tracks, scheduled for November 19
- **The score album** with 30 tracks, scheduled for December 11, 2026

## What the videos didn't reveal

Despite hundreds of millions of views, a few questions stay open until launch:

- Exactly how big the map is: Rockstar hasn't given a number.
- How long the story is.
- Whether and when an online mode is coming.
- Whether and when a PC version is coming.

## Our take

Rockstar staggered its marketing smartly: mood and characters first, then gameplay, story last. What stands out is how little the trailers gave away. We know the systems mostly from the Extended Look and the previews, and the plot only through hints. For anyone who wants to start spoiler-free, that's a gift. From tonight on, what's in the game is all that matters anyway.
