GTA 6 heads into The Game Awards 2026 as the favorite. When the nominations were announced on November 17, Grand Theft Auto VI landed in eight categories, more than any other game, including the biggest award of the night, Game of the Year. What makes it remarkable: at that point the game wasn't in stores yet and not a single review had been published.

**As of November 17, 2026.** The ceremony takes place on December 10.

## The eight categories GTA 6 is nominated in

| Category | Nominee |
|---|---|
| Game of the Year | Grand Theft Auto VI |
| Best Game Direction | Grand Theft Auto VI |
| Best Narrative | Grand Theft Auto VI |
| Best Art Direction | Grand Theft Auto VI |
| Best Score and Music | Grand Theft Auto VI |
| Best Audio Design | Grand Theft Auto VI |
| Best Performance | Manni L. Perez |
| Best Action/Adventure Game | Grand Theft Auto VI |

With Manni L. Perez, one of GTA 6's cast members is also in the running for Best Performance. She's up against Angela Sant'Albano (Resident Evil Requiem), Liam McIntyre (Marvel's Wolverine), Patrick Gibson (007 First Light) and Rahul Kohli (Saros).

## The Game of the Year race

Six games are competing for the top prize:

- 007 First Light
- Control Resonant
- Grand Theft Auto VI
- Phantom Blade Zero
- Resident Evil Requiem
- The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

According to GamesBeat's tally, GTA 6 leads with eight nominations, followed by Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem with six each, Phantom Blade Zero with five, and Zelda, 007 First Light and Big Walk with four each. The music and audio categories look especially tight: in Best Score and Music, GTA 6 faces Ace Combat 8 and Zelda among others, while Best Audio Design pits it against Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem.

## Why GTA 6 is eligible at all

For a long time it was unclear whether a November 19 release would count for this year's show. According to the official rules quoted by GamingBolt in October, a game has to be commercially available by Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 PM Pacific. GTA 6 launches one day earlier and squeezes in. Earlier reports suggesting the game would only be considered next year are now moot.

According to GamesBeat, the nominees are chosen by a global jury. How that jury assessed GTA 6 ahead of launch hasn't been made public. Reports say Rockstar started sending review code to media outlets back in late October.

## Our take

Eight nominations before the first review is a strong signal, but it's not a win. History shows Rockstar doesn't automatically sweep: at The Game Awards 2018, Red Dead Redemption 2 went in as a big favorite, yet God of War took Game of the Year. Rockstar did win awards that year in categories such as Narrative, Performance, and Score and Music.

Working in GTA 6's favor is that it's nominated in nearly every category that rewards a big open world with strong presentation: direction, storytelling, art direction and sound. Working against a clean sweep is the broad competition, especially Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem, which have six nominations each and go head to head with GTA 6 in several categories.

The real question is how the first reviews land. If scores are as high as for previous Rockstar games, GTA 6 should be the clear favorite on December 10. If critics push back, for example on the tech or the story, the race opens up.
